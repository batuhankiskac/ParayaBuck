# Guidance extraction specification — source design only

Extract one management EPS observation per issuer, fiscal target period, metric definition and public document version. Keep its document/event parent so simultaneous annual and quarterly guidance or GAAP and adjusted EPS do not become independent issuer opportunities by accident.

Required fields: issuer CIK, accession, document/exhibit identity, raw hash and text/table anchor, as-filed issuer/security identity, publication and observed timestamps, fiscal period label/start/end where verified, annual/quarterly horizon, currency, per-share unit, basic/diluted/ADR share basis, GAAP or exact non-GAAP definition, exclusions/reconciliation, shape, endpoints or point, approximation flag, action, predecessor link, and extraction adjudication. Missing required causal or comparison fields remain null with a reason.

Preserve actions separately: initial forecast, revised upward/downward, narrowed/widened range, reaffirmation, withdrawal, reinstatement and purely mechanical restatement. The issuer's word “raised” is not positive surprise versus analysts. An unchanged range may differ from the pre-event market expectation, but that requires the actual benchmark; do not infer it. Withdrawal is absence of a new numeric forecast, never zero EPS. Do not carry withdrawn guidance forward as current guidance.

Shapes: exact point, approximate point, closed range, one-sided bound and qualitative statement. Preserve negative signs and loss parentheses. Do not create an endpoint for “at least”, turn “low teens” into a number, or manufacture a tolerance around “approximately”. A midpoint is only a future literature-justified convention for a verified closed interval; none is calculated in this audit. Empty cells are not zero. Never treat table-wide millions as the EPS multiplier when the heading excludes per-share data.

Maintain a versioned chronological ledger. Identical release/exhibit/presentation reproductions share one observation; later filings never reset public time. Reaffirmation at a genuinely later public event is a state action, not a duplicate text hash to discard automatically. Amendments preserve earlier versions and become available at their own timestamps. Use contemporaneous predecessor evidence for revision classification; later reconciliation cannot improve earlier knowledge. Explicitly distinguish newly forecast quarters from completed-quarter actual results and preliminary actual announcements.

Benchmark joining must use an independently sourced analyst estimate snapshot strictly before the first public guidance disclosure, with matching issuer/security, fiscal period, currency, share basis, metric definition, contributor/aggregation method and vintage. Last pre-SEC acceptance is insufficient if a release or call was already public. Forecast revision versus previous management guidance, prior actual EPS, companyfacts or present-day consensus is not a replacement benchmark. No consensus observation exists in the current sample, so no surprise, ranking or signal was computed.

Future extraction validation would cover range ordering, ambiguous footnotes, two-period tables, duplicate exhibits, amendments, loss ranges crossing zero, splits, co-registrants and exact benchmark vintage. No collector or parser implementation is claimed. Unknown cases fail closed for eligibility while remaining in the coverage audit.
