# Proposed public graph contract (not executed)

Input: original 10-K accession and HTML/iXBRL (U.S. issuers); archive the raw bytes and hash, accepted datetime, form, fiscal period, and accession. Preserve filing vintage. Search Business, segment/customer notes, risk factors and contracts, but classify an edge only from exact cited contemporaneous text. A 10-Q can corroborate but would be an enhanced, separately labelled graph if used to update annual-base links.

Classification vocabulary: `NAMED_VERIFIED_PUBLIC_CUSTOMER`, `ANONYMOUS_MAJOR_CUSTOMER`, `GOVERNMENT_CUSTOMER`, `PRIVATE_CUSTOMER`, `UNRESOLVED_CUSTOMER`, `NAMED_NONLISTED_CUSTOMER`, `NAMED_LISTED_CUSTOMER_MAPPING_PENDING`, `NAMED_LISTED_CUSTOMER_VERIFIED`. `Customer A` is anonymous and never matched from industry context. The edge record requires issuer CIK, historical supplier security, exact customer legal entity/public parent and security, accession, fiscal period, accepted_at, source quote/hash, sales share if stated, and effective/superseded availability times. Duplicate supplier/customer edges within a vintage collapse with all citations retained; a later filing never repairs an earlier vintage.

Public graph is **not yet constructed**. Search-index examples show both named and anonymous disclosures, e.g. [Cirrus Logic FY2018 names Apple](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/772406/000077240618000007/a10-kxcrus53018.htm), [Hamilton Beach FY2023 names Walmart and Amazon](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1709164/000170916424000011/hbb-20231231.htm), and an [anonymous 2024 major-customer table](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1769624/000121390026062503/ea029006003-ars_triller.pdf). They are illustrative, selected search results, **not** a representative coverage sample or usable PIT edges.

A deterministic **candidate frame** of 30 historical-lifetime entities per calendar year (2016–2026; 330 rows) is frozen in `DETERMINISTIC_AUDIT_SAMPLE.csv/json`, using SHA-256 order before any relationship inspection. It has no CIK, SEC filings, or PIT size/sector strata yet, so it is not an audited issuer-year corpus. The required multi-sector filing corpus has not been fetched or manually validated. No denominator, public-link rate, graph fidelity, or monthly supply is reported.
