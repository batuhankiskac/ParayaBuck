# Gross and cash BUY headroom repair

The pure BUY and deferred BUY phases now call `PortfolioRuntime._size_buys_to_safe_capacity` immediately before durable order creation and broker submission. The gate reads exhaustive broker OPEN BUYs, then a fresh account snapshot. It combines marked absolute broker positions with nonterminal local BUY remainders and broker working BUY remainders, deduplicated by client order ID. Unknown or unpriceable working risk yields zero BUY headroom. The new candidate batch is scaled to the lesser of gross, cash-buffer, and (in LIVE) hard-dollar headroom; broker increments and minima are applied before persistence. A suppressed candidate does not become a durable phantom order. The global slot check is applied independently after sizing against the refreshed account.

Gross: `max(0, maximum_gross * current_equity - current_marked_gross - committed_buy_notional)`. Cash: `max(0, current_broker_cash - minimum_cash * current_equity - committed_buy_notional)`. Neither uses margin buying power. `cash_buffer_pct: 0.40`, `max_gross_exposure_pct: 0.6`, and all strategy settings remain unchanged. There is no continuous liquidation rule: mark drift can make realized gross exceed target, but new risk-increasing BUYs then receive `gross_headroom_exhausted`. SELL submission never enters this BUY gate.

The broker account and OPEN reads are authoritative snapshots rather than atomic reservations against external actors. Within this engine, the portfolio lock serializes account decisions; locally durable submitting orders reserve headroom before acknowledgement. A concurrent external trader can change the account after a read, so the next decision/reconciliation must re-sample broker truth. This is an operational limit of the existing broker interface, not permission to reuse a stale target.
