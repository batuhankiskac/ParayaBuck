# Parent attribution audit

A legal parent link does not authorize assigning the whole headline amount. For a wholly owned operating subsidiary, preserve both subsidiary recipient and listed parent and assess whether the contract could materially affect consolidated cash flows. For a 50/50 JV, consortium, minority stake or multiwinner vehicle, require a winner-specific allocated obligation or value; otherwise mark attribution unknown and exclude from ranking. Never copy an aggregate ceiling to every public winner.

The [2016 DoD shared IDIQ announcement](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/1011584/) describes an $11.4bn multiwinner ceiling with 17 firms and only $56,463 initially obligated in total. It is a concrete rejection example for 100% per-parent attribution. No sample event has a completed historical recipient-to-parent-to-security chain in this campaign; sample issuer names are observational leads only.
