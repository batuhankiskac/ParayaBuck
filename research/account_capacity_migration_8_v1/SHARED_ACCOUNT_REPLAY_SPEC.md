# Shared-account replay contract

Status: frozen research-only replay requirements, subordinate to `FROZEN_MIGRATION_SPEC.json` at freeze commit `3f7d0bc2b443f66b95d67ccb0503f1a0567799f5`. The two arms may differ only in the account-wide `risk.max_positions` value. This document explains implementation fidelity and cannot authorize a changed protocol after results.

## Why a new adapter is required

The existing product backtest accepts one market/strategy and filters bars to it (`src/parayabuck/backtest/product.py:276-383`). Its engine has one cash/position/pending state tied to that market and rejects a different market (`src/parayabuck/backtest/portfolio_engine.py:283-393,624-641`). It cannot measure cross-market slot competition by combining two standalone output curves. Its overlay call does not pass the runtime's reserved-position/open-order claims (`portfolio_engine.py:1059-1073`), and its `_apply_target` replaces pending orders (`:1233-1279`). Therefore a shared-cap claim from isolated replays would fail G7.

The minimal research adapter must drive both incumbent `PortfolioRuntime` instances through one deterministic simulated account broker and temporary portfolio repository. It may reuse production strategy, overlay, planner, runtime order state, and repository classes unchanged. Research code only owns causal historical feeds, simulated broker fills, event scheduling, telemetry extraction, and the account ledger. It must not alter `src/`, production config, PAPER/LIVE, or the frozen spec. Temp paths must isolate SQLite, cache, journal, snapshots and logs from production state.

## Causal unified clock

Input bars are not decision timestamps. Equity daily row labels are New York midnight labels; publish them after their trading session closes. Crypto UTC hourly bars aggregate to a strategy day only when all 24 contiguous hours are complete; that day's signal becomes available after its last hour ends. The scanner's PIT snapshot uses only data strictly earlier than its snapshot time. Merge those **availability** events into one UTC clock. If events tie, process crypto first, then equity, as runtime does (`src/parayabuck/runtime.py:3073-3120`). On a shared cycle, later decisions use the same pre-decision account snapshot and the earlier market's executable BUY reservations. No future bar may enter a signal, rank, universe, mark, or fill.

Start with a single $100,000 cash ledger, no positions/orders, and account equity equal to cash. Permit equity decisions from 2017-01-03 after causal warmup; keep the crypto sleeve in cash until 2021-04-01 and subsequent causal symbol eligibility. Never synthesize a cash transfer at crypto activation. End decisions on 2026-07-30; settle only against later eligible bars through the 2026-07-31 valuation cutoff. Mark remaining positions at the last causally available price, without forced liquidation. Orders lacking a later fill bar remain unfilled and are canceled for terminal reporting without costs.

## Orders, cash, and risk

At each decision, use one account snapshot of positions (including tiny nonzero and fractional quantities), account equity/cash, realized daily P&L/drawdown state, plus account-wide working orders. The actual production overlay counts all distinct `(market, canonical_symbol)` holdings and same-cycle reservations; V3 rank decides which equity targets survive count trimming (`src/parayabuck/execution/portfolio.py:263-348`). Keep the overlay's 0.60 gross, 0.40 minimum target cash, 0.55/0.05 active sleeves, 0.10/0.05 per-name caps, two crypto maximum, and daily-loss/drawdown gates. Fresh BUY execution must recheck actual cash and gross. Manual/external inventory rules still apply even if the replay starts without manual holdings.

Decisions use completed information; orders are submitted on that decision and fill no earlier than the next tradable open for their market. Sells must resolve before dependent buys, with fresh account and target replanning. Working orders and partial fills keep slot claims until terminal. Broker rejections, failed exits, and ambiguous statuses are not historically observable from OHLCV; deterministic synthetic fixtures must prove that the adapter does not release claims prematurely, invent fills, or transfer cash. Do not assign a historical frequency to those synthetic cases.

Apply the frozen 2 bps adverse slippage, 2 bps full spread, 25 bps crypto taker fee, effective-dated equity regulatory fees, commission zero, volume participation/increment/minimum and $10 order floor identically in A and B. Record each order intent, submission, fill, fee, status transition, account cash, position quantity, target and realized exposure, and mark. Attribute target shortfall at decision level by comparing pre-count eligible demand with post-count targets in the **same** account state; distinguish other eligibility, signal, liquidity, per-name, sleeve and gross limits. One account ledger is the sole source of NAV.

## Fidelity gates before economics

Focused synthetic tests must demonstrate six and eight count behavior; true cross-market count/crypto-first reservation; unresolved pending BUY and SELL claims; partial and rejected/failed exits; unchanged gross/sleeve/per-name limits; no synthetic cash transfer; causal bar availability; and byte-identical reruns. The replay must expose enough independent event detail to reconstruct decision counts, fills, cash/NAV accounting, costs and attribution. If this cannot be achieved without production behavior changes or a second parameter difference, stop the study as inconclusive rather than run historical outcomes.
