# Causality and daily execution
No execution adapter or candidate was implemented. Public guidance must be paired with a strictly earlier comparable consensus vintage. A delayed SEC filing cannot license analyst estimates issued after the earlier management release. SEC acceptance is a defensible observation time for a filing; it does not prove the first public instant when a release preceded filing. Missing earlier-release provenance blocks the benchmark join rather than allowing backdating.

A conservative daily mapping would use the first exchange session open strictly after proven public availability AND data readiness. A pre-open release may reach that day's open only when ingestion and all joined inputs are ready before it; a during-session or after-close release reaches a later open. Weekends/holidays use the actual session calendar, halts defer execution, exact-open ties defer, and unknown timezones/DST/date-only releases fail closed or require an explicitly justified later observation rule. This mapping is an engineering requirement, not a frozen strategy.

The current completed-bar engine rejects fills when `order.created_at >= bar.timestamp`; this establishes strictly later bar ordering, not event-ingestion correctness. A completed-close signal can conservatively defer a premarket disclosure further than its first available open. No claim of seamless immediate event support is made.

The final paper's principal three-month accumulation starts on the third day after the forecast; some regression prose/notes say second day. Next-open differs and can include an additional immediate-reaction window. Multi-month reported drift motivates compatibility investigation but does not prove executable next-open returns. Resolve this discrepancy before a future freeze; do not select the favorable clock from local outcomes.

Pre-outcome safeguards: identical accession/exhibit/event-period duplicates are one event; withdrawals and reaffirmations remain non-entry status records under the proposed scope; revised same-period and new-period forecasts remain separate records with no automatic position addition or expiry reset. At most one issuer exposure would be permitted. The exact replacement/expiry policy is deliberately not frozen after a non-READY decision. No prior-event accuracy uses outcomes unavailable at the new disclosure. No analyst response after disclosure becomes its expectation benchmark.
