# Paper variable definition

Per [author draft section 2.1 and Table 1](https://www.naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/2018_00M_Earnings-Acceleration-and-Stock-Returns_NAAIM-Submission-with-title-page.pdf): `EPS_t = IBQ_t / CSHOQ_t`, where Compustat `IBQ` is quarterly income before extraordinary items and `CSHOQ` is quarterly common shares outstanding; shares are adjusted for splits and stock dividends. This is a per-share *constructed* value, not automatically `us-gaap:EarningsPerShareBasic`, `EarningsPerShareDiluted`, or `NetIncomeLoss`.

`EGP_t = (EPS_t - EPS_(t-4)) / P_(t-1)` and `EAP_t = EGP_t - EGP_(t-1) = (EPS_t-EPS_(t-4))/P_(t-1) - (EPS_(t-1)-EPS_(t-5))/P_(t-2)`, with `P` the stock price at the end of the indicated fiscal quarter. Inputs are fiscal quarters `t,t-1,t-4,t-5`, prices at ends `t-1,t-2`, and point-in-time split factors. This is not percentage EPS growth and has no division by earnings, so negative earnings are permitted. Zero or nonpositive price, missing inputs, incompatible units, or ambiguous share/split basis fail closed.

Fiscal sequence must follow the issuer's fiscal calendar, including 52/53-week periods and year-end changes; calendar frames cannot identify it by themselves. Each earnings numerator is a discrete quarter, not a nine-month year-to-date value. Annual Q4 requires a separately published discrete-quarter value or valid FY less first-nine-month bridge, both known by the event. Source vintage is fixed as of decision time. The paper uses Compustat earnings-announcement date; an EDGAR `reportDate` or fiscal period end is not that date. The draft identifies a day-2 formation, but whether that means calendar or trading sessions and the exact open/close are not resolved by accessible text. No execution rule is frozen.

Published article: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165410119300333); detailed draft: [PDF](https://www.naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/2018_00M_Earnings-Acceleration-and-Stock-Returns_NAAIM-Submission-with-title-page.pdf).
