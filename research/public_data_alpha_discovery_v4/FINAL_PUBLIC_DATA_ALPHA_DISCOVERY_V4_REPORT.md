## Most important rejected families

- **Abnormal institutional ownership:** Kirk (2025 online / 2026 issue) reports a following-month 76 bp high-minus-low spread after a 45-day disclosure lag (external_published_evidence), but accessible evidence does not establish a standalone high long portfolio or a monotonic six-name tail. The characteristic model and 13F historical mapping also remain unresolved. [Primary article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0148558X251319189).
- **Phase III failures:** Afik, Lahav and Zaguri (2025) study 92 announcements; the long-friendly rebound is tied to small-firm failures, not validated liquid few-name deployment. [Primary article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612325013947).
- **Institutional breadth:** Chen, Hong and Stein (2002) report a diversified decile spread in mutual-fund holdings. The old primary method is not automatically replicated by public 13F, and no modern concentrated high tail was recovered. [Author manuscript](https://stein.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/breadth-ownership-and-stock-returns).
- **Dividend initiation/increases:** historical event drift is credible in the classic literature but modern liquid few-name strength evidence was not recovered. [Michaely et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1995.tb04796.x), [Bae](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1475-6803.1996.tb00229.x).
- **Special dividends:** prior V3 closure remains supported by Chou et al.'s lack of robust long-run performance outside smallest firms. [Primary article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1062976907000518).
- **Forward splits:** conflicting evidence and short-lived friction explanations prevent an execution-safe, concentrated post-announcement design. [Primary critique](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6288.2007.00180.x).
- **FDA Fast Track:** Williamson and Spicer report long-horizon effects for 25 biotech events in 2019–2020 (external_published_evidence). The sample is too narrow for a liquid, few-name, independent modern case, and first-public-announcement timing remains unresolved. [Primary article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359644623002878).
