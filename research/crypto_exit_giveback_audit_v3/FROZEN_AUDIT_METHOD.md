# Frozen V2 lifecycle diagnostic method for causal V3

Authority: immutable `archive/research/crypto-exit-giveback-audit-v2-2026-09`, `research/crypto_exit_giveback_audit_v2/EPISODE_DEFINITION.md`, `episodes.py`, and `INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.json`. This contract was read and fixed before computing V3 outcomes. The archived episode implementation is carried forward with changes limited to the corrected replay schema and physical event clock.

An episode is one symbol's flat → positive inventory → flat path. Adds, partial fills, partial sales, target changes and rebalances remain inside the same episode. Opening is the first actual buy fill. Closing is the actual last sell fill that removes inventory. A natural close comes from ordinary strategy orders. Inventory held at the source-common endpoint is separately censored, settled by the precommitted terminal events, and excluded from natural-exit rates.

Economic P&L at each completed daily mark is cumulative net sale proceeds minus cumulative gross purchase outlay including buy fees, plus remaining quantity times the completed close. Sell fees reduce proceeds. Fill price already embeds spread/slippage; these are not charged again. The fixed denominator is **all gross purchase outlay including buy fees across every add**, determined ex post. Final return = final net P&L / that denominator.

MFE is the maximum marked economic P&L while inventory is active, from completed daily closes only. The first timestamp wins a tie. Intraday highs and actual flat settlement are excluded from MFE. Positive MFE means MFE > 0. Profit retention = final net P&L / MFE when MFE > 0; otherwise null. It is not clipped at 0 or 1. The >=30% cohort means at least one active completed mark with marked P&L / fixed denominator >= 0.30. The cohort is restricted to natural closures for its final-profit rate and median retention. No alternative threshold informs the decision.

Corrected-clock adaptation: daily snapshot `available_at` is the physical time when the completed-bar mark exists. Actual hourly fill timestamps define starts, closures and durations. The last corrected snapshot is post-terminal and flat. Terminal liquidation occurs before the new daily bar is ingested, so the final active mark is the preceding completed daily snapshot. The terminal event's `last_close` is the close of the hourly execution bar and is excluded from MFE because it was unavailable at the terminal open. The independent verifier checks all prior marks, the terminal fill, and final account reconciliation. No later daily signal is consumed. Partial sale proceeds stay in episode P&L. A forced terminal settlement remains censored even though inventory is flat in the final replay state.

Holding duration is actual first-buy to final-sale physical time. Distributions use linear interpolation at P10/P25/median/P75/P90, as in V2. Retention bins are >=75%, [50%,75%), [25%,50%), (0%,25%), <=0%. Final profitable means P&L > 0; winner-to-loser means positive MFE and final P&L < 0. Symbol breakdown is descriptive only.

**MFE is not an executable exit signal.** No exit is chosen at MFE; no perfect peak capture or alternative exit is simulated.
