# Long-only evidence

Classification: LONG_HIGH_EVIDENCE_LIMITED.

[Pyun's publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176525001727) explicitly describes positive cumulative returns and market outperformance for strategies based on firms receiving large contracts. This is directional evidence for the high leg, independent of the stated high-minus-low relation. It does not disclose an absolute high-leg estimate, market-adjusted high leg, value-weighted high leg, large-cap high leg, long-horizon high leg, or their significance. Those six numerical items remain UNKNOWN because the paper's tables were inaccessible. The abstract cannot establish whether a concentrated top tail works.

The [TenderAlpha defense announcement white paper](https://www.tenderalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/White-Paper-Defense-Contract-Announcements-TenderAlpha-HKU-1.pdf), p. 5, reports that its top 5% by absolute contract dollars has three-day market-adjusted VW 12.5 bp (t=1.22) and EW 13.6 bp (t=1.33), and five-day VW 13.8 bp (t=1.05) and EW 12.8 bp (t=0.99). Those are external published results, not local calculations, and not conventionally significant. It is a different global defense dataset and horizon, so it neither refutes nor verifies Pyun's long-horizon high leg.
