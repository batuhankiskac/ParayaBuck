# One-slot feasibility

## Six-slot architecture

The six-position limit is account-wide and counts cross-market positions and claims. V3's configured equity allocation is 0.55; crypto is 0.05; gross is 0.60. Six equal equity names at 0.55 are about 0.091667 each and fit the 0.10 per-equity cap only in an equity-only view. Two occupied crypto slots leave at most four equity slots.

The durable 5+1 policy permits a research comparison of five V3 names plus one satellite at 0.091667, with explicit exclusion, one owner per instrument, shared ledger, next-bar fills, and unchanged crypto. It does not establish that six equity positions fit concurrently in the existing PAPER account.

## 13D one-slot thesis

A single satellite would require a defensible rule for choosing among overlapping campaigns. The literature does not supply one. Taking every qualified event conflicts with one slot; selecting the largest stake, strongest demand, or best-known activist would introduce an unvalidated rule. Sparse cash is acceptable in principle, but cannot repair missing rank validity.

## Verdict

`ONE_SLOT_13D_THESIS_UNSUPPORTED`

The engine can represent a bounded satellite after a candidate is frozen, but economic and selection evidence do not justify freezing one.
