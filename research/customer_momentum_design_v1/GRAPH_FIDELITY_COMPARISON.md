# Graph fidelity status

Published Cohen–Frazzini sample: mean 918 suppliers/year, 433 customers/year, mean/median link duration 2.7/2 years, about 23% same-industry pairs; 30,622 firm-year links total ([Table I and methods](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf)). These figures are **not** targets to tune a public graph to.

Public graph: none accepted. Named-public-customer coverage, annual edges, customer-sales shares, industry mix, duration and overlap are `NOT_MEASURED`. The proposed public edge clock may differ from Compustat's annual last-available link; no equivalence or `PUBLIC_GRAPH_NOT_FAITHFUL_TO_PRIMARY_SAMPLE` finding can honestly be asserted yet. The design gate remains blocked by absence of a validated graph, not a measured low coverage rate.
