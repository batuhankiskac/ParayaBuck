# Fourth-quarter accounting audit

Annual 10-K values often span the full fiscal year. A theoretical discrete Q4 bridge is `FY_IBQ - first_9_months_IBQ`, with share treatment requiring a *separately justified* quarter-end `CSHOQ` and split-adjusted consistency; subtraction of annual and YTD EPS is generally unsafe because EPS denominators differ. This does not establish that the Q4 result was known at the earlier earnings release.

Q4 passes only if a contemporaneous 8-K/issuer release directly reports an exact comparable quarter value and shares, or if all bridge components were public at the decision time and match the paper definition. Annual filing, amendment, restatement, 53-week shift, fiscal-year change, or inconsistent share basis can alter a later reconstruction. The cached sample did not retrieve historical EX-99 releases, so Q4 derivability and availability are `UNVERIFIED`; ambiguous Q4 events are excluded, not backfilled.
