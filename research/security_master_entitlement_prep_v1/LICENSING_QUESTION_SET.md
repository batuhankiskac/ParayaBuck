# Licensing question set — NYSE Group Security Master — 2026-09-22

Use case to state to the vendor (only): historical internal quantitative research /
point-in-time security classification, one user. No redistribution requested.
This is product-document review plus open questions, not legal advice.

## L1 — Archive licensing model

L1.1 Is 2015-12-16–present archive access included with a current subscription or sold separately
(one-time extraction fee, separate historical product)?
L1.2 Is every equity full AND delta file from 2015-12-16 bulk-retrievable (SFTP/S3), or are there
date-range, rate, or per-file limits?

## L2 — Permitted research use

L2.1 Is internal quantitative research and historical backtesting expressly permitted under the
entitlement (including non-display use)?
L2.2 May we create internal derived effective-dated classification tables (e.g. ORDINARY_COMMON,
ETF, ETN, PREFERRED, WARRANT) from the files for personal/internal research?

## L3 — Storage and retention

L3.1 May original historical files, checksums, and backups be stored locally (single machine /
private backup) while subscribed?
L3.2 May derived effective-dated tables be retained indefinitely, including after subscription
termination, if they contain no redistributed raw vendor fields?
L3.3 May ORIGINAL files be retained after termination, or must they (and which copies/backups) be
destroyed? State deletion obligations and certification, if any.
L3.4 Do NOT generalize Cboe's purchased-file perpetual-retention term to NYSE: require the NYSE
term in writing.

## L4 — Identifiers and third-party fields

L4.1 Are there contractual restrictions on storing/displaying CUSIP, ISIN, or proprietary Stock
Number (including separate CGS/FTSE licenses or fees)?
L4.2 If CUSIP/ISIN/FTSE rights end, what must be removed: raw fields only, or derived tables
built from them?

## L5 — Users, devices, contractors, cloud

L5.1 What user/device/contractor/company limits apply to a one-user internal-research entitlement?
L5.2 Are local backup and single-region private cloud storage permitted, or is use restricted to
named hardware?

L6.1 Confirm no redistribution is included. May aggregate research results (no raw fields, no
per-security vendor dumps) be published or shared?
L6.2 May derived taxonomy labels (our own category names) be described in methodology notes without
reproducing vendor field values?

## L7 — Evaluation terms

L7.1 Is an evaluation/trial entitlement available without contractual acceptance beyond the sample
terms, and may evaluation bytes/hashes plus derived audit counts be retained?
L7.2 Do evaluation files carry the same redistribution prohibition as production files?

## L8 — Pricing (one-user internal research)

L8.1 Quote: one-time historical backfill fee; recurring daily-update fee; any evaluation fee;
any identifier (CUSIP/ISIN/FTSE) surcharge.
L8.2 Confirm the quote excludes redistribution rights we do not need.
