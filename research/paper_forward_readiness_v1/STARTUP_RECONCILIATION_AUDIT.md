# Startup reconciliation barrier
EngineRuntime._start_impl calls broker.reconcile before creating PortfolioRuntime, starting workers, warmup, catch-up, or accepting decisions. Drift sets a circuit breaker and halted state; exceptions emit reconciliation_failure and prevent start. Offline synthetic coverage: tests/execution/test_reconciliation_repair.py covers full/partial BUY and SELL fills, cancellation/rejection, missing/wrong orders, unknown positions, malformed FILL pages, repeated pages, incomplete FILL history and restart equivalence. tests/test_restart_chaos.py covers flat, managed inventory, open BUY/SELL, partial fill, and crash recovery. tests/test_dual_market_startup.py covers two-market startup. No broker request was made in this audit.
External positions can be left unmanaged yet still appear in broker-wide account exposure/slots. Unexplained ownership or quantity drift may halt startup. This is a fail-closed synthetic/code conclusion, not a live broker acceptance result.
