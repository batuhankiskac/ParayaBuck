# Event definition audit

Retrieved 2026-09-22. Scope: source and event semantics only; no local candidate outcomes inspected.

## Narrow observable event

A candidate event is a **U.S. public parent distributing shares of a separately identifiable U.S. operating-company child pro rata to parent common holders, without holder consideration, followed by that child's ordinary common shares commencing regular-way trading on a U.S. national exchange**. The parent and child issuer, specific share class, distribution ratio and percentage, record date, effective distribution date, and regular-way date must each have a public, time-stamped source before the decision. This is an investigational *definition*, not a frozen investable strategy. SEC Staff Legal Bulletin No. 4 defines the usual no-sale pro-rata conditions, including no consideration, adequate disclosure, business purpose, and treatment of restricted securities; it is a securities-law condition set, not evidence of alpha. [SEC Staff Legal Bulletin No. 4, 1997](https://www.sec.gov/interps/legal/slbcf4.txt).

The strict 100%-of-wholly-owned-child version is the closest match to the sample of 172 U.S. full spins closed 1988–2012 in [Tara Bhandari, SEC DERA working paper, November 2013, Table 1](https://www.sec.gov/files/dera-wp-differences-opinion-evidence-spinoffs.pdf). Yet two directly inspected modern examples are **partial**: 3M distributed 80.1% of Solventum and retained 19.9%; Illumina distributed 85.5% of GRAIL and retained 14.5%. [3M's archived release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/66740/000006674024000023/pressrelease3-8x24.htm); [Illumina's completion release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1110803/000119312524167025/d851030dex991.htm). These do **not** prove that partial spins share a full-spin return effect. Record them as a separate cohort unless primary long-only evidence authorizes pooling. A strict 100% rule may materially reduce supply; that count remains unmeasured.

## Transaction boundaries

| Form | Treatment in strict evidence-matched cohort | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Pro-rata tax-free spin of 100% of a wholly owned U.S. operating child | Potential inclusion, subject to PIT identity and regular-way proof | Matches the full-spin exemplar population; confirm the tax claim in the then-current filing, rather than infer from the label. |
| Pro-rata taxable distribution | Separate/unknown | Taxable status can change holder pressure; no basis here to pool. |
| Pro-rata **partial** distribution with retained parent shares | Separate/unknown | Observable (SOLV, GRAL), but not the 100% DERA sample. |
| Split-off/exchange offer | Exclude | Holders surrender parent shares by election; non-pro-rata selection differs from the SEC's pro-rata no-consideration structure. |
| Carve-out IPO followed by a later distribution | Exclude from newly listed-child cohort; separately assess only if later distribution is in scope | Child already had public price/history before the distribution. |
| Tracking-stock separation | Exclude until a distinct operating child issuer and distributed ordinary share class are proved | Tracking stock may remain a parent security. |
| Merger-related distribution | Exclude | Immediate merger conversion may eliminate a standalone tradable child; an [SEC example](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1861622/000149315226038333/form10-q.htm) shows spun shares converted into merger consideration. |
| Rights, units, warrants or preferred distribution | Exclude | Different security and payoff; not ordinary child common. |
| REIT common separation | Separate/unknown | REIT common is permitted by repository taxonomy, but the operating-company mechanism and literature transfer are unproved. |
| Foreign parent/child or ADS/ADR distribution | Separate/unknown | The SEC bulletin itself has foreign-company-specific disclosure treatment; issuer domicile and instrument differ. |

At decision time, an announced proposal, filed Form 10, registration effectiveness, or record date alone is insufficient evidence of completion. A contemporaneous completion filing or issuer release plus the separate regular-way listing evidence is required; otherwise status is `UNKNOWN/INELIGIBLE`. Later bankruptcy, takeover, index inclusion, subsequent sale of retained shares, and subsequent performance must never change the original cohort label. This separates the child post-distribution question from generic spin-off research; it does not establish the desired child return result.
