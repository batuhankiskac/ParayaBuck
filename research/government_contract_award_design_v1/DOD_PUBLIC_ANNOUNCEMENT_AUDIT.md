# DoD public announcement audit

DoD has two distinct channels. Its FPDS CARs are held 90 days after Date Signed under [PGI 204.604](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfarspgi/pgi-204.6-contract-reporting). Its [public contract archive](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/) has dated daily announcements for large actions. The [2014](https://www.defense.gov/Today-in-DoD/Date/2014-4-16) and [2020](https://www.defense.gov/News/Today-in-DOD/Date/2020-06-27/source/GovDelivery/) official Today in DoD pages describe a 5 p.m. Eastern publication schedule, while [FAR 5.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/5.303) controls pre-release. This is schedule evidence, not proof that every archived page first became public precisely at 17:00.

[DFARS 205.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/subpart-205.3-synopses-contract-awards) covers modifications and special IDIQ/undefinitized treatment, uses face value excluding unexercised options for announcement threshold, and allows FMS entries. A [2016 Boeing announcement](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/793556/) shows $667.5m headline versus $136.1m obligated. A [2026 Lockheed IDIQ](https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4575681/contracts-for-aug-17-2026/) shows $31.3m headline versus $297,008 obligated. A [2026 Boeing modification](https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4599224/contracts-for-sept-11-2026/) increases a ceiling by $13.4bn with zero obligated. A [2016 shared IDIQ](https://www.defense.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/1011584/) announces a collective $11.4bn ceiling for 17 firms and only $56,463 combined initial obligations. These cannot be assigned equally or fully to parents.

A DoD-only corpus is visible and plausibly causal under a conservative next-session rule, after checking earlier public disclosures. It is a sector-biased subset of Pyun's broader federal sample, with changing thresholds and no demonstrated exact-paper event equivalence.
