# Execution feasibility audit

**Gate status: `CONDITIONAL_SOURCE_AND_LIQUIDITY_PROOF_REQUIRED`.** The existing listed-equity execution model can represent a regular-way ordinary share order after a completed decision bar, with a fill proxy at the next tradable bar's open. It does not itself prove which historical bar was the first regular-way session or that a historical child was correctly mapped. [Source: `src/parayabuck/backtest/portfolio_engine.py:273,880-882`; `src/parayabuck/backtest/product.py:399`; `docs/research/canonical_v3_baseline.md`.]

An executable event would need an issuer or exchange source specifying the **actual** regular-way start for the identified child share class, preserved with public timestamp. The earliest permissible order must be after both (a) the public event and mapping evidence were available and (b) normal regular-way trading began; execution would occur at the next eligible open after that decision. Calendar arithmetic from distribution date is not an acceptable substitute. When-issued prices, auction-only mechanics, pre-distribution appreciation, and same-open fills based on post-open discovery cannot be credited. This is a required design constraint, not a currently verified historical source capability.

Current production scanner rules require active tradability, non-OTC listing, price at least **$5**, trailing 20-day median dollar volume at least **$20 million**, data completeness at least **0.95**, and ATR percentage in **[0.005, 0.12]**. These rules can substantially delay or exclude a new listing until trailing observations exist. No spin-child attrition or spread distribution can be calculated before a verified event-to-security inventory is built. The scanner's spread input affects quality ranking but has no hard positive maximum-spread rejection in its equity filter. [Source: `src/parayabuck/config.py:340-348`; `src/parayabuck/universe/scanner.py:174-205,293-319`.]

The current product backtest proxy uses 2 bps adverse slippage and 2 bps spread at baseline, zero commission, effective-dated equity regulatory fees, a 0.5% rebalance band, and a $10 minimum order. These are existing assumptions, not measured post-spin transaction costs. A sparse or small-cap child cohort may have wider opening spreads and lower depth. With 6–12 month holdings turnover might be modest after entry, but that duration and event arrival rate have not been established by this audit. No costs were recomputed and no economic simulation was run. [Source: `config/settings.yaml:186-201,227-228`; `src/parayabuck/backtest/product.py:353-381`.]

**Conclusion:** Standard listed shares and next-open orders are mechanically compatible with the platform. Historical regular-way identification, exact ordinary-share mapping, initial scanner eligibility, and realistic opening liquidity remain unverified. Execution feasibility therefore cannot independently clear a design-ready decision.
