# SEC source capability audit

Authority: [SEC EDGAR API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) describes `data.sec.gov/submissions/`, `api/xbrl/companyfacts/`, `api/xbrl/companyconcept/`, and nightly `submissions.zip`/`companyfacts.zip`. Company Facts contains standard, entity-wide taxonomy facts, excluding custom-tag and dimensional data. SEC says submissions are typically refreshed in under a second and XBRL in under a minute, with longer peaks. [SEC FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) says public documents often lag acceptance by 1-3 minutes or more and no exact first-public-content timestamp is provided. Acceptance is a lower bound, not proved `available_at`.

The 8-K Item 2.02 may furnish an earnings release as EX-99.1, while later 10-Q/10-K reports are a different information event. [SEC Inline XBRL guidance](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/structured-data/inline-xbrl) requires 10-Q/10-K statements but does not imply all Item 2.02 earnings tables are structured. An [SEC example filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1090727/000162828026003510/0001628280-26-003510-index.htm) has iXBRL 8-K cover plus separate EX-99.1/99.2 earnings documents; cover tagging cannot be mistaken for earnings-fact tagging.

`STRUCTURAL_EVENT_SUPPLY.json` audits 14 pre-existing cached issuer Company Facts and *recent* submissions arrays, 2016-2026, without returns. Among its visible Item 2.02 rows, zero have same-accession standard Company Facts facts, while sampled 10-Q rows usually have `EarningsPerShareBasic`. This is a convenience sample of large firms, early years omit archived submissions segments, and it does not examine EX-99 documents. It demonstrates the risk of using later 10-Q facts at the earlier announcement; it does not estimate full-market coverage or disprove extractability from release tables. Exact IBQ/CSHOQ coverage, custom-tag-only counts, Q4 bridge, and issuer release time remain unmeasured. No SEC network request was made because the required declared `SEC_USER_AGENT` was absent; cached source hashes are in the JSON.

To complete a source-feasibility campaign: load archived submissions and raw 8-K exhibits for a representative historical issuer sample; verify exact IBQ-compatible earnings and shares against release and 10-Q; retain content hash, accession, acceptance and observed dissemination; quantify by year and excluded class. A full-market claim is prohibited on this sample.
