# FPDS and USAspending availability audit

[FAR 4.604](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/4.604) requires complete ordinary CAR submission within three business days after award; specified urgent actions have 30 days. The [2012 FAR amendment](https://www.acquisition.gov/reg-change-notice/fac2005-62) confirms this rule predates the proposed 2016 development period. A completed CAR is not necessarily publicly released on its action date.

[USAspending's official data guide](https://www.usaspending.gov/data/about-the-data-download.pdf) says FPDS submissions reach USAspending the next morning and the site publishes them the following day. [DoD PGI 204.604](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfarspgi/pgi-204.6-contract-reporting) withholds DoD CAR data from public/non-DoD use until 90 days after Date Signed. A [September 2016 DoD PGI archive](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/pgi/pgi_htm/r20161021/PGI204_6.htm) is search-indexed with the same 90-day rule; direct archived-host retrieval failed, so this historical verification is slightly weaker than the current rule. SAM's [Contract Awards API](https://open.gsa.gov/api/contract-awards/) also documents public DoD reveal only after 90 days.

These are policy windows, not measured first-public times. We verified no same-PIID, same-modification, historical source pair with both exact publication timestamps. SOURCE_DELAY_DISTRIBUTION.json therefore records no empirical distribution. A date-signed or USAspending action date is forbidden as available_at. A conservative later public record may be usable only with archived proof of that actual vintage and a frozen entry rule.
