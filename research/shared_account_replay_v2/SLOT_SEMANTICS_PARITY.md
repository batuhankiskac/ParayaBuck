# Global slot parity

The current production helper `reserve_opening_order_slots` admits ordered BUY requests against a set of nonzero positions and reserved/working opening keys. V2 invokes it after cross-market decisions and when held BUY intents activate after the sell phase. A market-tagged key prevents symbol-only collision in the synthetic ledger. A held-symbol add is admitted without a new claim. A SELL intent, pending SELL, partial SELL, or band-suppressed exit leaves the positive position present and occupying its slot. Full exit frees a slot only after the position reaches zero. A pending new BUY reserves a slot; opening fill replaces that reservation with an occupied position, deduplicated by key. Cancellation removes the pending claim exactly once. `tests/test_shared_account_replay_v2.py` includes six-slot cases for full account, five plus reserved, two competing openings, held add, suppressed LTC-style exit, cross-asset identity, and cancellation. The historical combined run must separately show zero over-cap timestamps.
