# Concentration feasibility

The raw EAP is a natural descending rank. The [author draft Table 2](https://www.naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/2018_00M_Earnings-Acceleration-and-Stock-Returns_NAAIM-Submission-with-title-page.pdf) shows an approximately increasing **across-decile** return profile, and section 2.2 calls it monotonic. This supports selecting a high decile, not ordering expected returns *within* that decile. The paper's scaled regressors replace each value with a decile rank, so their significant slope cannot validate choosing the top 1, 3, or 6 raw EAP values. No within-decile tail curve, concentrated basket, single-name risk analysis, or top-N evidence was found in the primary accessible paper. Extrapolating the decile relation to six names would add an unsupported tail hypothesis.

Current `config/settings.yaml` caps global positions at eight and crypto at two, leaving at most six for all equity ideas alongside V3; equity V3 itself has `top_n: 8`, 0.55 sleeve and 10% per-name limit. A diversified top decile cannot fit without an arbitrary truncation and displaced incumbents. The cached 14-issuer source sample lacks causal signals and mapped securities, so weekly candidate counts, clustering, slot-time and V3 collision rates cannot be measured. The faithful month-long horizon is day 2-30, with a quarter-long alternative ending after next earnings; choosing shorter duration to save slots is not authorized.

Disposition: `DESIGN_REJECT_NO_DEFENSIBLE_CONCENTRATED_EXPRESSION`. This is a design-evidence verdict, not evidence that earnings acceleration has no diversified alpha. It prevents freezing a top-N rule and local economic testing under the requested concentrated long-only mandate.
