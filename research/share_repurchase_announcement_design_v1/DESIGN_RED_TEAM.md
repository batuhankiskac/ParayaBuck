# Design red team

Pre-outcome review, 2026-09-22. Evidence findings are qualified by the primary
and modern literature reviews; none is a local economic result.

| Challenge | Assessment before economics |
|---|---|
| 1. Most return immediate? | Announcement effects cannot be assigned to a later open. Long-horizon supportive evidence also exists, so immediate-only is not established universally. |
| 2. Persistent drift? | Contested; later reauthorizations/takeovers can create step changes rather than continuous correction (completion review). |
| 3. After 2000/2010/2020? | Contradictory post-2000 studies; some samples extend beyond 2010. Modern publication dates are not post-2020 sample evidence. Required next-open modern liquid-stock proof is absent. |
| 4. Large/liquid firms? | Aggregate value-weighted weakness and small/high-volatility concentration restrict transferability. |
| 5. Equal-weight small caps? | A material concern explicitly reported in supportive persistence research; no broad inference from equal-weight portfolios to one liquid issuer. |
| 6. Cheap talk? | Authorization permits but does not obligate execution. Keep stated mandate separate from realized purchases. |
| 7. Future completion needed? | Do not overclaim: unconditional announcement selection needs no advance completion knowledge. Ex-post completion/takeover winners cannot become an initial selector. |
| 8. PIT source coverage? | Primary examples prove discoverability, not exhaustive 2016–2026 capture. Full-corpus coverage remains blocked/unmeasured. |
| 9. Form consistency? | 8-K items/exhibits and retrospective periodic reports differ. No single-item assumption survives. |
| 10. Repeat dedup? | Accession is a filing ID, not a program ID. Require issuer/security/program linkage and retain lineage of incremental authorizations. |
| 11. Natural rank? | Prior decline is the strongest simple lead reviewed; no supported modern top-one/top-six implementation is established. Published composites do not satisfy the one-variable requirement. |
| 12. Market-cap dependency? | Size-normalized authorization requires PIT shares/market cap, absent locally. Today's shares are inadmissible. Prior decline avoids this but does not solve concentration evidence. |
| 13. Long slot duration? | 12–48 month evidence commits scarce capacity for one to four slot-years per holding. No externally justified alpha density supports that commitment. |
| 14. One-slot expression? | Issuer specificity makes it conceivable, not validated. Supportive portfolios are much broader. |
| 15. V3 overlap? | Disclosure input differs, but subsequent momentum may overlap. Conventional factor controls do not prove local V3 complementarity. |
| 16. Earnings contamination? | Real filings bundle earnings and repurchases. Record bundling before economics. |
| 17. Separate earnings surprise? | No demonstrated PIT consensus/expectations source or independent attribution method. No outcome-selected exclusion allowed. |
| 18. Takeovers? | Later takeover gains contribute in published decomposition; neither future targets nor non-targets may be selected with hindsight. |
| 19. Next-open misses effect? | It misses prior reactions by construction. No verified cost-controlled concentrated study measures exactly this causal clock. |
| 20. Publication decay? | Disappearance and persistence results conflict. Classic evidence alone is insufficient; no categorical claim of universal anomaly death. |

## Decision discipline

Reject the requested concentrated hypothesis on the evidence reviewed, not
because a local backtest failed and not because the anomaly must be zero.
Data/source/mapping blockers are additional limitations. Fixing acquisition
alone would not produce a supported ranking, horizon or one-slot thesis.
No candidate, alternative selector, horizon sweep or economic protocol is
frozen. A future reconsideration requires new independent pre-outcome evidence.
