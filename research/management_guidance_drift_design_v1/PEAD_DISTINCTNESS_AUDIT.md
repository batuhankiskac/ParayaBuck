# Guidance versus earnings news and momentum

Verdict: `GUIDANCE_SURPRISE_INCREMENTAL_TO_EARNINGS_NEWS`, scoped to the external evidence reviewed. This does not certify causal isolation in every bundled release or complementarity to local V3.

## Exact final Ng, Tuna and Verdi evidence

The published paper excludes guidance within three days of an earnings announcement (p.962). It therefore does not provide a bundled-EAD versus non-EAD portfolio comparison. Its Table 4 controls beta, size, book-to-market, prior momentum, earnings surprise and analyst forecast revisions; Table 5 independently sorts PEAD terciles and guidance-surprise quintiles and finds guidance drift within the PEAD groups. Those are meaningful checks against merely relabeling previous earnings news. [Final published paper, pp.962, 973–978](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-012-9217-4).

Momentum is a prior 12-month raw return ending two months before the forecast month. Prior analyst drift uses consensus changes one and two months before the forecast, rather than analysts' reactions afterward. These controls differ from a local V3 residual-momentum signal and from a contemporaneous bundled earnings-surprise correction. The paper's regression text/table notes also contain clock and mean/median inconsistencies documented in the primary signal review; do not silently call them an exact live execution contract.

## Independent bundled-disclosure evidence

Milian (2018) compares quarterly guidance and actual earnings released together. The published abstract finds distinct post-announcement timing: roughly half the guidance-related drift occurs at the next earnings announcement, while earnings-related drift partly reverses then. The publisher's notes discuss a conditional guidance-news measure that accounts for updated expectations caused by concurrent earnings. This supports distinguishable information in guidance, rather than an assertion that bundling makes separation impossible. Some announcement-window hedge comparisons require pre-news positions and are explicitly not earnable strategies. [Final article abstract and notes 4, 6–9](https://doi.org/10.1080/09638180.2016.1231074).

This follow-up is not permission to change the candidate to a conditional composite or adopt its different prior-quarter breakpoints. A literature-faithful future replication must preserve the chosen Ng sample semantics or declare and independently justify any departure before outcomes.

## Implications for this campaign

- Management guidance surprise compares management's disclosed future EPS with the matching expectation before disclosure; actual earnings surprise concerns realized EPS; analyst revision concerns analysts changing forecasts. Retain distinct fields and provenance.
- Do not add actual earnings surprise, V3 score, price momentum or credibility into the selection score.
- An issuer disclosure supplies a distinct input channel; conventional momentum controls do not prove low local outcome correlation or one-slot portfolio benefit. No such local calculation was performed.
- For bundled releases, preserve earnings/guidance flags and metric/period matches. A PIT analyst panel and explicit joint-news treatment remain necessary; no analyst reaction occurring afterward may be used as the initial expectation.

The positive enum reflects evidence of incrementality, not proof that all PEAD confounding has been eliminated for every source/period. The modern-investability gate remains separate.
