# Metric, fiscal period and share-basis audit

A valid comparison matches what management forecast to what analysts forecast. Neither a universal GAAP-only rule nor a generic “adjusted EPS” label proves comparability. Store definitions and adjustment exclusions at the event vintage; a provider's street-EPS methodology must match or provide an auditable contemporaneous bridge. Without that evidence, fail closed rather than subtract unlike quantities.

[Boeing's 2016 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000095012316020852/a201609sep308kpressrelease.htm) gives separate GAAP and core ranges and describes a favorable tax adjustment. That demonstrates why a larger forecast need not mean stronger recurring operations. [InterDigital's release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1405495/000140549524000071/ex991q133124pressrelease.htm) separates diluted and non-GAAP EPS for both Q2 and the full year, with differing forecast assumptions. An annual number cannot be compared to quarterly consensus, and reported Q1 actuals are not Q2 guidance. Non-GAAP labels across companies are not interchangeable.

[Amcor's release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1748790/000174879026000004/exhibit991q22026.htm) targets the year ending June 30, 2026. Its $4.00–4.15 range reaffirms $0.80–0.83 after a 1-for-5 reverse split effective January 14; treating that as a forecast increase would be a mechanical error. Its forward non-GAAP reconciliation is unavailable. This does not impose automatic exclusion of all non-GAAP guidance, but leaves basis matching unproved. No analyst snapshot was obtained to resolve it.

[PG&E's joint report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1004980/000119312524268378/d861932d8k.htm) requires distinguishing parent-company EPS from utility securities and matching 2024 versus 2025 targets. [B/E Aerospace](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/861361/000094787116001362/ss4067_ex9901.htm) uses approximate point language; preserve that uncertainty rather than pretending a closed interval was supplied.

Required comparison checks are fiscal period start/end and annual/quarterly duration; 52/53-week or fiscal-calendar transitions; currency and currency-neutral assumptions; units and loss signs; basic/diluted denominator; ordinary share/ADR ratio; split effective date and adjustment vintage; continuing/total operations; acquisition scope; and the actual non-GAAP exclusions. The sample retains unresolved fiscal end dates as null instead of inferring every issuer is calendar-year. An as-filed dollar sign should be resolved to a verified currency before production eligibility. Today's split-adjusted databases cannot silently rewrite source-vintage EPS.

No current shares outstanding, price-derived scaling, prior realized EPS or hindsight reconciliation is used to repair a missing analyst benchmark. Metric alignment, timestamp alignment and security mapping are independent mandatory data gates, all before any surprise calculation.
