# Takeover and outcome dependence

## Unconditional versus future-conditioned evidence

Greenwood and Schor show that long-horizon gains are concentrated among targets acquired after activism. Their independent-target estimates are small and statistically weak; “no news” independent targets have no +18-month abnormal return. Becht et al. likewise find that return realization is concentrated around observable outcomes and especially takeovers, while no-outcome engagement performance is weak or negative.

An acquisition, board settlement, capital return, restructuring, strategic change, or successful proxy campaign is not known from the original filing. Using it to select, rank, hold, or retrospectively label a winning entry is direct lookahead.

## Permitted treatment in any future falsification study

Outcome and takeover fields could be recorded only after the fact for attribution and concentration diagnostics. They could not:

- determine entry eligibility;
- choose among simultaneous filings;
- extend a holding period until success;
- remove failed campaigns;
- define an “activist quality” score from later success.

## Gate verdicts

- `ACTIVISM_RETURNS_REQUIRE_FUTURE_OUTCOME_INFORMATION`
- `RETURNS_LARGELY_REQUIRE_FUTURE_TAKEOVER_OUTCOME`

These are rejection conditions. The premium is not shown to survive as an unconditional filing-time long leg.
