# Anti-redundancy audit

Every Stage-A family was compared with the repository register and archived V3 table. Dividend initiation and recurring increases are distinct from V3 special distributions; Phase III trial outcomes are distinct from FDA approval holder continuation; abnormal 13F ownership and breadth differ from V3 short-interest and insider ownership. Forward splits are distinct from spin-off child drift. Backlog and license grants differ from government contract awards. No prior closed family is promoted to the shortlist.

Special cash dividends stayed excluded: newer accessible primary evidence still reports no robust post-declaration long-run result except the smallest firms. Annual/quarterly text-change families did not enter Stage A because V3 already screened MD&A/risk additions and new disclosure novelty. No `NEW_SOURCE_REOPENING_EVIDENCE` was established.
