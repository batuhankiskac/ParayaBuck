# Licensing and entitlement audit

This is a factual product-document review, not legal advice.

| Product | Subscription/history | Delivery | Public price | Material unresolved rights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NYSE Group Security Master | Entitlement required; historical files documented from 2015-12-16 | SFTP/S3, daily full/delta | `COMMERCIAL_QUOTE_REQUIRED` | bulk backfill, internal backtest, indefinite raw/derived retention, termination, backups, contractors/users, redistribution, CUSIP/ISIN/FTSE fields |
| Nasdaq Daily List + Fundamental Data | Agreement/application/approval; Daily List history since 1999; full master publicly promises current month only | secure FTP/website | $3,500/month/organization in official 2024 notice/filing | bulk archive method/fee, intraday versions, research/backtest, derived tables, termination, users/contractors, CUSIP license |
| Cboe BZX reports | Order form/data agreement; history and subscription separately selectable; CUSIP version separately entitled | SFTP/Snowflake | `COMMERCIAL_QUOTE_REQUIRED` | exact backtest rights, hashes, multi-user environment, publication of derived labels, CUSIP after termination |
| SEC sources | Public with fair-access controls | HTTPS bulk/API/filings | Free | respect SEC access policy; source completeness remains limited |

Cboe documentation distinguishes purchased non-index historical file retention (perpetual) from Snowflake copies (generally destroyed on service termination). That fact must not be generalized to NYSE or Nasdaq. Technical cloud storage capability is not contractual storage permission.

No vendor was contacted, no form was submitted, no terms accepted and no purchase authorized.
