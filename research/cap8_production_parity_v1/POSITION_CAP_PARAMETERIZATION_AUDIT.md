# Account slot parameterization

Production config is `max_positions: 6` (`config/settings.yaml:184`). `RiskLimits` validates positive `max_positions` and `max_crypto_positions <= max_positions` (`src/parayabuck/config.py:210-239`); mapping carries the configured global cap into `PortfolioRiskLimits` (`execution/portfolio.py:112-125`). Overlay account-wide fixed slots include marked positions, reserved keys and broker working-order symbols; available slots derive from `self.limits.maximum_positions` (`portfolio.py:266-307`). `reserve_opening_order_slots` uses its passed parameter and does not credit an unfilled SELL (`portfolio.py:556-586`). Runtime passes its risk limit in immediate and deferred flows (`runtime_service.py:1088-1094,1448-1455`); the coordinator accumulates cross-market reservations (`runtime.py:2483-2507,3518`). Broker reconciliation and account snapshots are collection based, not six-element arrays. Dashboard reads configured max positions (`web/server_legacy.py:3081`).

The synthetic cap 6 test blocks a new opening with seven occupied positions, while cap 8 admits the opening. This demonstrates parameter plumbing, not gross safety. Existing `tests/test_global_slot_accounting.py` exercises count six; the audit fixture explicitly exercises eight. A later PAPER cap change must keep crypto cap 2, verify live restart reconstruction and apply the separate reconciliation repair.
