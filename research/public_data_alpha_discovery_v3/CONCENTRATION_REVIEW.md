Rule: never infer decile-works → top-one-works. Each family: literature expression size →

## A1 Spin-child drift (SHORTLIST)

- Literature expression: event-time portfolios of tens of concurrent spin-children
  (10–30 eligible in most months), held 6–36 months. Diversified across spins, not deciles.
- Top-six: PLAUSIBLE. 10–30 concurrent eligibles fill 6 diversified slots most months;
  recency × liquidity ranking averages single-spin idiosyncrasy while keeping the neglect
  tilt. Monthly top-6 equal/inverse-vol within the reserved sleeve is the default expression.
- Top-one: WEAK but documented. Most-recent liquid child maximizes the neglect mechanism
  but carries single-event variance; a 1-slot satellite variant is described only, with
  top-6 as default. No literature supports a single-spin rule.

## F-1 Short interest (REJECT_EVIDENCE; concentration also fails)

- Literature: 99th-percentile tails (~55/5,500 names) or deciles, rebalanced monthly.
- Top-six: NO. No paper supports a 6-stock low-SI-surprise thesis; low-SI is the modal
  condition (median stock ~1% shorted), so the filter is a near-market basket without a
  tight conditioner. Median highly-shorted spell 2–3 months → monthly turnover.
- Top-one: NO. Single low-SI name carries no documented alpha.

## General lesson (consistent with register)

Broad factor evidence (deciles, value-weighted spreads, announcement pops) repeatedly lacks
the alpha density per occupied slot ParayaBuck needs. Only issuer-event families with
10–30 concurrent naturals (here: spin-children) can fill 6 slots without forcing
diversification into weak names.
