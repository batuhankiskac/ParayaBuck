# Observability
The dashboard and runtime health expose PAPER mode, LIVE badge, account information, workers, scanner status, active universe, reconciliation status, holdings, gross/cash, pending orders, decisions/fills and buy capacity. capacity_health reports global effective slots and cap 8; dashboard risk reports crypto held positions and cap 2. Crypto effective slots including working orders are enforced by the order gate but are not displayed as a dedicated dashboard count. Operator can compare broker OPEN orders and crypto positions; this is a visibility limitation, not evidence of an over-cap execution.
The material gap is receipt and soak audit completeness (see their dedicated audits). A health snapshot is an atomic JSON heartbeat, not a substitute for order/fill proof. Sources: runtime.py:655-713; web/server_legacy.py:3060-3120; monitoring/snapshot.py.
