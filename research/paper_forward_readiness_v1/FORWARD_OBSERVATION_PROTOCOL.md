# Frozen operational observation protocol, version 1
Freeze before any PAPER outcome. Minimum duration: 14 consecutive calendar days, with at least two naturally occurring weekly equity opportunities and at least three 96-hour crypto opportunities; extend without changing thresholds if market/calendar data prevents those counts. Existing 1–2 week guidance is retained at its conservative two-week end. Start clock only after both scanners, reconciliation, workers and readyz are healthy.
Pass requires zero unexplained drift, duplicate broker client order IDs, effective global claims above 8, effective crypto claims above 2, BUYs violating fresh 0.60 gross/0.40 cash, unresolved ambiguous orders, or missing decision/order/fill lifecycle records. Require complete receipts for both markets and startup/scheduled origins, successful scanner recovery after a naturally observed transient, and one supervised graceful restart with reconciliation-first continuity. Measure uptime as ready/expected time and disclose any interruption; target at least 99% ready time, excluding documented planned restart minutes. Retain every incident and audit result.
Record P&L for description only. Do not change V3/crypto parameters, allocations, cap8, crypto cap2, rebalance band or risk thresholds based on short-term P&L. Operational bug fixes require a separate evidence/revalidation note; strategy changes require a separate research campaign.
