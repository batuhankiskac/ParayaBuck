# Independent interpretation review

`MIXED_PROFIT_RETENTION_BEHAVIOR` is the best descriptive conclusion. The evidence does not establish systematic collapse of large winners. Under the requested gate that a new exit campaign opens only when that concern is genuinely supported, recommend `CRYPTO_EXIT_RESEARCH_NOT_JUSTIFIED`. This means this audit does not justify opening that campaign; it does not establish that current exits are optimal.

The natural-exit sample contains 55 episodes; the full account contains 57 including two terminal liquidations. Among 50 natural episodes with positive completed-bar MFE, 20 ended negative (40%). But only 3 of 27 reaching +10% and 1 of 19 reaching +20% ended nonpositive; all 15 reaching +30% remained profitable. Median retention rises from 60.37% in the +5% group to 75.27%, 85.35%, and 87.15% in the +10%, +20%, and +30% groups. This concentration of round trips among modest gains differs materially from the claim that typical large winners collapse.

Among the 15 large winners, 11 retained at least 75%, three retained less than half, and one retained less than a quarter. ETH entered April 2021 is the clearest large-winner erosion case: +66.60% MFE to +13.05% final, retaining 19.60%. LTC entered February 2024 went from +42.06% to +20.99% (49.92% retention). The other large LTC episodes retained 75.12%–98.42%. A historical LTC example cannot identify the user's current PAPER holding.

## Limits and remaining integration

Completed-bar MFE is hindsight and peak profit was not necessarily tradable. Normalized episode returns use the artifact capital denominator and are not an investor buy-and-hold return. Retention may legitimately exceed one or be negative and is not clipped. Aggregate mean retention is unstable for tiny positive MFE. No alternative exit was tested, designed, ranked, or simulated.

Enriched decision paths show 25 natural episodes with post-peak retained-only exposure versus 30 without. Their median givebacks are 581.65 versus 93.02, median retentions -49.09% versus 75.27%, and median peak-to-exit delays 34 versus 4 days. This association merits documenting but is confounded by path length and selection; it does not establish avoidable loss or typical large-winner collapse. Rank-based retention extends through rank four and is evaluated on a 96-hour scheduled cadence; there is no individual four-day holding lock. Verified initiating exit reasons are 38 rank failures, four retention-capacity displacements, eight qualified-history losses, and five scanner-ineligible exits; two terminal liquidations are censored. The recommendation remains MIXED_PROFIT_RETENTION_BEHAVIOR and CRYPTO_EXIT_RESEARCH_NOT_JUSTIFIED under the requested genuinely-yes gate.
