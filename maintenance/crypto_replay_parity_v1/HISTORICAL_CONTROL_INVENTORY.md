## Limits of historical verification

The old audit proves research and production classes match **within the same engine and inputs**. It is not an independent verifier of that engine. Its `ranking_history` is selected-symbol ordering used as a proxy, not the complete volatility ranking. The persisted file stores hashes rather than the full historic target/fill ledgers. Reconstructing those ledgers at the historical SHA is required for exact first-divergence analysis. The old comparison logs artifact metrics but does not make artifact metric equality part of its overall pass boolean.

All archive tags listed locally concern later equity/design research; no crypto-specific archive tag exists. Their inherited old crypto evidence is recoverable from pre-cleanup commit 90988e7. Commit `8123694` removed the old research artifacts and runners; deletion does not invalidate their Git history.
