verdict
  The screen is WORKING as a filter and is NOT too strict: a real after-tax edge of **+0.25 pp/yr**
  is detected at Stage 1 and passes the whole Stage 2 holdout (both the d = 0.25 and every larger
  drift arm), pure noise and a pre-2013 edge that vanished are correctly rejected, and the four
  positive controls reproduce their campaign verdicts (R2 vs E2 and K vs A1u pass Stage 2; A1u vs
  R2 and K vs R2 fail on the pre-tax bootstrap gate G5, the documented owner-accepted tension).
  The campaign's 0/303 is NOT a power failure: no real arm reached Stage 2 at all (245 reject_stage1,
  19 reject_stage2, 8 not_applicable, 1 holdout_insufficient, 0 stage2_pass) because the idea bank's
  median arm is -0.70 pp after tax versus K.
  The ONE broken piece is the **deflation's deflated Sharpe**: its `var_sr` is the cross-sectional
  variance of the trial Sharpes (~1.07 on the campaign pool, 360x the null sampling variance
  0.00135), so its threshold sr0 is 1.82-6.69 -- larger than the best real arm's Sharpe (0.55) and
  even larger than the +2.00 pp/yr arm's (5.30). Under the framework's rule NOTHING is declarable,
  not even a 2 pp/yr edge. Correcting `var_sr` to the null sampling variance `(1+SR^2/2)/(n_obs-1)`
  (Bailey & Lopez de Prado's own quantity; PROPOSED amendment, PREREGISTRATION SS7.2) declares the
  five drift arms; it changes NO real arm's verdict (none passed Stage 2).

what was wrong in v1 / v2
  v1: the positive controls' "declared" came from a **1-arm** batch deflation (var_sr = 0 -> sr0 = 0,
  a degenerate pass); the committed results/DEFLATION.json is only for pc_synth and declares []. So
  the v1 report's declaration column is unsupported. v1's synthetic power scaled the *weights*
  (+5/+10 % exposure), not a return edge, and put the "null" in the weights -- it measured the wrong
  object and found no Stage-2 power.
  v2: the drift is an **env-global** (GATE_POWER_DRIFT): one batch-level drift applied to every
  synthetic leg, so `syn_null0` was an exact copy of `syn_edge` and `syn_noise_p/m` were K clones
  (the noise seed defaulted to -1, so no noise was ever applied). v2's whole synthetic-power table
  is therefore invalid, and its Stage-2 minimum-detectable-edge (+2 pp/yr) is wrong: the corrected
  per-arm drift shows Stage 1 and Stage 2 detect +0.25 pp/yr (v2 overstated the MDE ~8x). v2's
  claim that the screen "declares a +2.0 pp/yr edge" is false under the framework's own deflation --
  the +2 pp/yr arm is NOT declared (sr0_framework 6.69).
