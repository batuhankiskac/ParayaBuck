# Concentration feasibility

No top-N, quantile, award threshold or holding occupancy is frozen. Pyun's abstract names larger contracts and stronger large-cap effects, but the actual sorting breakpoints, continuous coefficient, large-cap definition, weighting and holding period remain inaccessible. The [TenderAlpha white paper](https://www.tenderalpha.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/White-Paper-Defense-Contract-Announcements-TenderAlpha-HKU-1.pdf) finds no conventionally significant short-horizon top-5%-absolute-dollar effect and its relative-to-market-cap result is a different signal. DoD's announcement threshold is a disclosure rule, not an externally validated investable strength threshold.

The bounded five-record official sample is deliberately chosen across years and event types; it cannot estimate qualifying monthly issuer counts, repeated signal overlap, simultaneous slot demand or tail supply. Without a full event corpus, historical parent mapping and a paper-faithful holding horizon, no conclusion that the signal fits six scarce account positions is valid. The existing V3 target cadence is weekly with maximum six account positions (docs/research/canonical_v3_baseline.md); that account limit does not justify selecting six award names.
