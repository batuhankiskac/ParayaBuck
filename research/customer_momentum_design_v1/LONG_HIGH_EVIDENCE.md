Classification: `CUSTOMER_MOMENTUM_LONG_HIGH_LIMITED`. There is independent positive historical evidence, including a later high decile. Evidence for the **specific** broad quintile, liquid, few-name, public-graph expression remains unproven: the primary value-weighted five-factor high alpha is not significant, Table V's liquid/large rows report spreads rather than independent high-leg alpha, and the later decile is a different breadth. No high-only large/liquid later-period result was located. This is a constrained hypothesis, not a G2 failure proven by the spread alone.
