# Broker positions versus local ownership

Broker quantity, average entry price, current price, cash, and equity come from \`get_account\`/\`get_all_positions\` (\`alpaca_account.py:130-251\`). Local ledger/repository quantities determine \`managed_qty\`; any residual is \`external_qty\`. Absent local lineage therefore does **not** authorize the strategy to sell a broker-only position. The risk overlay treats external inventory as immutable while counting its market value in gross/sleeve constraints (\`execution/portfolio.py:141-225\`). \`manage_external_positions\` is \`false\` in current configuration (\`config/settings.yaml:211\`) and default config (\`config.py:244\`); the reconciler does not install stops on external positions in that configuration (\`alpaca_reconciliation.py:476-485\`).

Broker-only position: \`BROKER_STALE\`/\`MISSING_LOCAL_ORDER\` drift, startup halts. Local-only position: stale ownership closure/adjustment is attempted, but \`LOCAL_STALE\` was already recorded and startup halts. Quantity mismatch: \`QUANTITY_MISMATCH\` or \`OwnershipDriftError\`, not silent broker/local overwrite. Average-price mismatch: broker average is used in \`PositionSnapshot\`; local realized/entry accounting remains ledger-derived. Mixed holdings keep both broker total and local managed portion. Unknown/manual OPEN orders also create \`UNKNOWN_ORDER\` drift; ordinary startup fails closed instead of trading through them. Evidence: \`alpaca_account.py:143-251\`; \`alpaca_reconciliation.py:391-617\`; \`runtime.py:910-947\`.

An externally introduced over-cap state is counted by risk/slot logic; the bot cannot erase it and further opening BUYs are denied. It may prevent all startup decisions through \`BROKER_STALE\` until ownership/policy is reconciled.
