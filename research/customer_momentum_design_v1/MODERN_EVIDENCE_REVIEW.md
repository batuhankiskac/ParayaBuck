# Modern evidence and its limits

- [Pinchuk, *Customer Momentum* (2023 working paper)](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.11394) repeats a Compustat/CRSP customer-return design through 2018. Its 2005–2018 long-short decile alpha is not significant at 5% under most factor specifications, while the high decile has positive FF5+UMD alpha: equal-weighted 0.50%/month (t=2.32), value-weighted 0.61% (t=2.07). This is basic replication with mixed post-discovery support, not a public-source graph replication.
- [Cen, Hertzel, and Schiller, *Speed Matters* (2025)](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2023.00291) studies diffusion speed and attention shocks. It supports a plausible transmission mechanism; it does not establish a profitable free-data long-only high-customer-return portfolio.
- [*Psychological barrier and cross-firm return predictability*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X21002725) studies interaction with the supplier's 52-week high. It is a modified strategy/mechanism test, not the Cohen–Frazzini base. No 52-week-high filter enters V1.
- Network centrality variants change the signal and often use licensed supply-chain data. They do not validate the exact base high quintile or a few-name public graph.

Classification: `LIMITED_MODERN_SUPPORT`. No identified work in this review proves the specific 2016–2026 public/free-data, PIT, concentrated, long-only implementation.
