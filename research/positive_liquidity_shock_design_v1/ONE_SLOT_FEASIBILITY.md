# One-slot feasibility

## Verdict

`ONE_SLOT_ALPHA_DENSITY_UNSUPPORTED`

The original decile relation is broadly but not strictly monotone. Neither the
primary paper nor a reviewed replication shows that the single maximum `LIQU`
observation has reliable alpha density. Extreme observations can instead be
the most sensitive to price-impact denominator noise and issuer events.

Every successful external expression is diversified. No issuer-level case,
top-one order-statistic test or production-like reserved-slot study supports a
single monthly pick. The canonical future 5+1 policy would allocate
`0.55/6 = 0.0916667` to one satellite with a five-slot V3 core, but this
architecture cannot create evidence absent from the literature.

The one-slot gate therefore fails before local economics.
