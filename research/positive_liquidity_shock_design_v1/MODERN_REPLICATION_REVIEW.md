# Modern and external replication review

## Verdict

`MODERN_LIQUIDITY_SHOCK_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`

There is modern support for a broad international liquidity-shock phenomenon,
but not for the required claim: a post-publication U.S. large/liquid,
concentrated, positive-shock long-only strategy.

## Direct and related evidence

- [Jang (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1111/ajfs.12360) directly reconstructs the
  shock in Korea. The high decile has positive alpha and the effect survives
  momentum, reversal, IVOL, illiquidity, turnover, size and abnormal-volume
  controls. However, the most-liquid quintile spread is negative; the effect
  is concentrated in illiquid stocks.
- [Iwanaga and Hirose (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pacfin.2022.101849)
  find positive-shock continuation in Japan from 1995–2020. Their mechanism
  evidence emphasizes illiquidity more than the original U.S. attention
  channel. This supports the broad phenomenon, not exact U.S. implementation.
- [Lee and Wang (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jempfin.2023.04.005) report
  international long-short predictability across 40 countries through 2020,
  but do not isolate post-2014 U.S. liquid-stock long-leg performance.
- [Han, Hu and Huang (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2023.102517) find
  U.S. illiquidity shocks retain information after IVOL shocks, supporting
  distinctness from IVOL but not concentrated long-only implementation.
- [Feng, Kang and Zhang (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jimonfin.2023.102966)
  find modern international asymmetry: liquidity decreases drive the strong
  predictability, while positive liquidity increases are small and mostly
  insignificant. This directly cautions against translating the spread into a
  positive-shock-only strategy.

## Required inference

The original paper's large/liquid/S&P 500 subsamples are encouraging but end
in 2010. Korea provides adverse liquid-stock evidence, and the strongest
modern asymmetry evidence says the negative side dominates. No reviewed source
validates a top-six or one-slot expression. External evidence therefore fails
the campaign's modern/liquid/concentration gate.
