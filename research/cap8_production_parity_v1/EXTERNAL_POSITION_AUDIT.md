# Manual and external inventory

The overlay treats unapproved and external quantities as immutable exposure and fixed slot claims (`execution/portfolio.py:181-307`); a mixed position retains its external floor and managed portion separately. Pure external symbols cannot be sold or grown by this runtime (`portfolio.py:240-263`), and an external per-name excess is surfaced as `external_exceeds_asset_limit:<symbol>`. The planner sells only managed quantity (`portfolio.py:425-554`). A one-external plus seven-managed account fills cap 8; an additional opening is denied by account-wide slot admission. The audit's one-external plus six-managed fixture at cap 6 denies a new opening, while at cap 8 the same account admits a BUY despite gross 0.605. Thus external accounting is present but the order-headroom defect remains. No real manual/broker position was changed.
