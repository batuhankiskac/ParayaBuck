# three_leg_kernel_v1 -- the N-leg kernel (N <= 4) for the batch-screen framework

**Verdict: built and parity-proven. Engineering only -- no idea's performance was computed.**

`research/fast_engine3/` generalises `fe2_sim.sim2` to N <= 4 instruments in one cash pool (per-leg FIFO books and costs, the A1u band / H2 deferral applied per leg on that leg's own book, one Turkish taxpayer) and adds the margin loan of `research/fast_engine_opts` (interest on a negative cash balance). `research/fast_engine/` and `research/fast_engine2/` are not edited: the per-leg primitives (`_buy_leg` / `_sell_leg`) and the tax engine are imported unchanged.

## The framework harness

`python -m research.batch_screen.run --harness`: PASS (139 checks, 0 failed) -- the published 116 cells plus the multi-leg routing cells; `research/batch_screen/HARNESS.json`.

## What is now expressible

* IC36 A/B/C -- a 3/4-instrument inverse-vol blend in the off state, rescaled monthly
* IC34-C -- QLD* with XLU and XLP held at once
* IC45-C / IC46-C -- a sleeve on top of production past the 0.98 cap, funded by a cash loan with interest
* IC41 A/B/C -- QLD* plus the S&P 500 2x leg (`2x_spy`) as leg B

## What remains blocked

* two books of the same instrument (one FIFO queue per symbol per taxpayer)
* more than 4 instruments in one account
* a per-session instrument switch inside one leg
* several cash pools / accounts, or a per-arm tax regime
* the single-leg execution options (band_up, slicing, the DD governor, T+1, FX cost, withholding) on a multi-leg arm
