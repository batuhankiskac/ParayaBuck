# Causality audit

The proposed mechanism is investor recognition/attention: unusually high own-stock activity may expand the investor base and induce delayed demand. Later real-side work is consistent with financing and investment responses. These are mechanisms compatible with the evidence, not identified causal facts.

Competing explanations include same-day price pressure, information arrival, disagreement, short-sale constraints, liquidity demand, announcements, and product flows. The design addresses only part of this set:

- own-history ranking reduces permanent size/liquidity-level contamination;
- the GKM middle-40% formation-return restriction reduces contemporaneous return-extreme contamination;
- next-open entry prevents trading on an unavailable closing observation;
- the shared scanner/risk/execution controls make the V3 comparison interpretable;
- split-invariant dollar volume avoids mechanical split shocks.

It does not observe investor identity, news, order direction, borrow, spreads at historical depth, or common-stock taxonomy. It therefore cannot label a realized premium as caused by visibility.

The candidate is predictive research only. Permitted language is “abnormal-dollar-volume signal” or “consistent with attention/visibility.” Forbidden language includes “causal attention alpha,” “proven investor recognition,” or any claim that volume itself causes returns.

Verdict: `PREDICTIVE_HYPOTHESIS_CAUSALLY_UNRESOLVED`.
