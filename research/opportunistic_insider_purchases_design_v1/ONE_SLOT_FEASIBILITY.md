# One-slot feasibility

Verdict: not demonstrated.

CMP supplies issuer-month measures such as numbers of opportunistic buys and
sells and also examines transaction intensity relative to shares outstanding.
Those are research variables; the campaign found no externally validated,
long-only, modern, liquid-stock rule that says which single issuer should win a
scarce ParayaBuck slot.

Raw purchase dollars are not a safe substitute: Form 4 price may be absent or
footnoted, Code P includes private purchases, and dollar size is not the CMP
classification rule. CEO/CFO bonuses, cluster points, direct-ownership points,
and blended conviction scores are likewise unsupported.

Even if a natural ranking were later established, the position limit is
account-wide. Two crypto holdings can reduce available equity capacity to four;
passing the equity-only 5+1 comparator would not prove deployment capacity.

No ranking, tie-break, maximum occupancy, or cash-redeployment rule is frozen
in this blocked campaign.
