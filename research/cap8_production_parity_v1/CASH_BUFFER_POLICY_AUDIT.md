# Cash buffer semantics

`cash_buffer_pct: 0.40` in `config/settings.yaml:183` constrains risk-adjusted expected post-trade target via `1-minimum_cash` (`src/parayabuck/execution/portfolio.py:350-363`). `docs/research/canonical_v3_baseline.md:35-68` explicitly distinguishes target cash from realized `cash/equity` and states there is no continuous drift liquidation. Deferred buys additionally check available broker cash (`runtime_service.py:1576-1580`), while pure buys have no equivalent fresh sizing. In the no-leverage frozen trace, `gross = 1-cash/NAV`; cap 8 has 9,152 marks below 40% realized cash, minimum 38.4492546%, exactly the gross-over-60 mark set. Cap 6 has zero and minimum 41.0232833%. This drift alone is consistent with target-cash policy; order-caused cash consumption while already below target is the separate defect.
