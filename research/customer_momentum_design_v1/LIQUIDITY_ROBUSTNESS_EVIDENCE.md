# Liquidity evidence

The original sort excludes supplier stocks with prior-month-end price <= $5. [Table V](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~afrazzin/pdf/Economic%20Links%20and%20Predictable%20Returns%20-%20Cohen%20and%20Frazzini.pdf) defines “liquid” as positive volume on every trading day in the preceding 12 months, and “larger” as above the contemporaneous CRSP median market cap. It reports liquid-stock and larger-stock **long-short** spread robustness (VW 1.464% and 1.475% monthly, respectively, in the baseline one-month-return column). Those are not stand-alone high-leg alpha estimates. Table V Panel A has some high-leg raw excess/alpha columns under relative-size/turnover/coverage restrictions, but they do not establish a liquid high leg under ParayaBuck's scanner policy.
