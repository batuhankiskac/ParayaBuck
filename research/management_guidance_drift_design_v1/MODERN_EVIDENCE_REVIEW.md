# Modern US guidance-drift evidence

Review date: 2026-09-22. Primary-source search and published-result interpretation only. No local prices, candidate returns, or economic experiments.

Verdict: `MODERN_US_GUIDANCE_DRIFT_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` for affirmative readiness of the requested contemporary concentrated implementation. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that the anomaly disappeared or that a future experiment must lose.

## Final-paper baseline and temporal caution

Ng, Tuna and Verdi's final 2013 paper studies 1996–2008. Its Figure 2 narrative reports generally positive calendar-quarter hedge returns with some economically substantial negative quarters. A full-text search and inspection did not establish the prompt's suggested explicit time-decay result in this final version; no decay coefficient or early/late contrast is fabricated. Publication in 2013 does not extend observations past 2008. [Final published article, Table 1 and Figures 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11142-012-9217-4).

## Direct post-2008 supportive follow-up

S&P Capital IQ's January 2016 primary research studies Russell 3000 companies during January 2003–February 2015. Its official release reports positive-consensus guidance followed by 1.3% one-month and 2.5% three-month excess returns; a good-news portfolio retains positive performance after market, size, value and momentum controls. This is meaningful independent practitioner evidence extending beyond 2010, not merely citation of the older paper. [Official research description](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/what-does-earnings-guidance-tell-us); [official findings](https://press.spglobal.com/2016-01-06-Positive-Pre-Announced-Corporate-Earnings-Guidance-Has-Significant-Upside-Impact-on-Stock-Performance-S-P-Capital-IQ-and-SNL-Research-Finds).

The vendor paper also studies prior-guidance revisions, so its combined good-news portfolio is not an exact replication of the final Ng signal. The retrieved report warns that reported performance excludes fees. Its 2003–2015 pooled result is not isolated post-2010, post-publication, or 2017–2026 proof. No top-one, six-name, large/liquid-only, or exact next-open net-cost result was independently verified. PDF text extraction was incomplete; only directly available vendor statements and indexed primary text are used. [Primary report](https://www.spglobal.com/content/dam/spglobal/mi/en/documents/general/what-does-earnings-guidance-tell-us-listen-when-management-announces-good-news.pdf).

No qualifying post-2020 US replication of the exact positive-surprise next-open long leg, with modern large/liquid coverage and realistic costs, was verified in this bounded search. Absence from the retrieved set is not proof none exists. Faster parsing, broader dissemination and changing guidance frequency are reasons to demand current evidence, not substitute evidence of zero alpha. The good-news mechanism remains plausible; data and implementation gaps should be recorded separately from economic rejection.
