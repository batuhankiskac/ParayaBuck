# One-slot rule reassessment

## Verdict

`ONE_SLOT_RULE_NOT_DEFENSIBLE`

The structural audit finds at least one fail-closed opportunistic purchase in
all 111 months from 2017-01 through 2026-03. Median supply is 19 transaction
rows/month; the range is 1 to 147. There are 1,225 distinct entity-month
opportunities. Therefore a single equity slot is structurally fillable
under this source/mapping subset.

That does not establish a ranking rule or economic feasibility. CMP Appendix
A9 finds that the log count of opportunistic buys is predictive in diversified
portfolio tests, but it does not test top-one/winner-take-all selection. The
literature reviewed here does not show that concentrating into one issuer
preserves the result, survives costs, or fits the account-wide six-position
constraint when crypto positions occupy capacity.

Accordingly, monthly occupancy is a descriptive count only. No top-one score,
tie-breaker, or candidate is frozen.
