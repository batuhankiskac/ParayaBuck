# Public availability audit

Only one sample SEC Accepted field is directly verified: [Amcor accession 0001748790-26-000004](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1748790/000174879026000004/0001748790-26-000004-index.html), accepted 2026-02-03 16:12:44, filing date February 3. Preserve the raw text and Eastern timezone (`America/New_York`) when normalizing. This is not proof of the earliest release distribution. All six earliest-public-instant fields remain null.

For every event retain issuer release time, SEC acceptance, dissemination time/evidence, observation time and earliest verified public instant separately. Release date, fiscal period end, signature and report date are not timestamp substitutes. PG&E's specimen has November 29 report date and December 2 signature date; neither establishes its first public instant. A dated current issuer page does not establish archival intraday provenance.

The [SEC access guide](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) notes that some submissions starting after 17:30 ET disseminate next business day. Pair Accepted with dissemination validation or a conservative verified availability bound; do not assume instantaneous availability for every filing. Corrections/removals require versioned snapshots. Other sample acceptance fields are unknown after failed index retrievals, not zero or midnight.

Guidance timing has two distinct causal gates. Execution must occur at a regular-session open strictly after validated public availability and ingestion readiness. Separately, the analyst benchmark must be demonstrably before the first public guidance statement. Using a later SEC time can be conservative for trading yet contaminate consensus with post-release analyst updates. A conservative execution delay does not repair that benchmark contamination.

If an issuer release or conference call preceded the SEC filing, capture primary archived evidence or fail closed. If only a date is known, do not assert first-open execution or a pre-event consensus cutoff within that day. Missing timezone, unresolved late dissemination or conflicting public times block eligibility. No intraday timing optimization, execution-date computation, price lookup or returns were performed. Daily compatibility remains a design condition requiring evidence of persistence beyond the first executable open.
