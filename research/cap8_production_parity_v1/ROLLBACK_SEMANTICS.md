# Cap 8 to cap 6 rollback semantics

`reserve_opening_order_slots` starts from every nonflat broker position and reserved/open-order key, and only adds a new opening when `len(slots) < maximum_positions` (`execution/portfolio.py:556-586`). It never liquidates to satisfy a reduced count; a requested SELL does not release a slot until a fresh zero-position snapshot. Overlay counts immutable/manual inventory and existing positions, and can choose managed target exits on later decisions (`portfolio.py:266-307`). Consequently an account with seven or eight held positions after a cap-6 rollback is over the opening cap, but existing inventory remains until strategy/rebalance exits; new openings are blocked. Operator rollback must handle pending opening orders using broker reconciliation before restart. Immediate forced liquidation is not established policy and is not part of this plan.
