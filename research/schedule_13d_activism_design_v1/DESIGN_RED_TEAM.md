## Independent rejection case

Even if authoritative SEC parsing and mapping were funded, the strategy would still fail the requested evidence gate: no robust unconditional outsider post-filing long leg, material future-outcome dependence, and no natural one-slot ranking rule. Therefore a data acquisition project would not rescue this hypothesis as presently stated.
