what remains blocked, and why
  * IC36_N/B_N (and the bank's IC36-B): the {GLD, IEF, SHY, XLU} blend is leg A + FOUR sleeves =
    5 instruments in one cash pool; the N-leg kernel's capacity is N_LEGS = 4. Needs N_LEGS >= 5
    (a kernel edit + parity), not a plugin.
  * IC41/A_N, B_N, C_N (and the pinned IC41/A-C): the arms are *defined* by the tax router -- a
    downsize fills in the leg with the lower USD FIFO gain fraction, bounded by a 10 pp per-session
    cap. That router exists only as the two-leg fill option `route_cap_pp`
    (research/fast_engine2/fe2_opts.py); `api.resolve_arm` refuses `fill_knobs` on any `"legs"` arm
    (only `margin_spread` reaches the multi-leg kernel) and `fe3_sim.sim3` has no cross-leg routing
    (checked in the code). Screening the 50/50 weight split alone would silently drop the mechanism
    the idea exists for, so it is not done -- the arms are registered with that reason.
  * The 8 old arms of these ideas (IC34/C, IC36/A-C, IC41/A-C, IC45/C, IC46/C) keep their pinned
    `needs_kernel` ledger lines. IC34/C, IC45/C and IC46/C are pinned only by the ledger text --
    their runnable twins are C_N here. IC36/A-C are also blocked by IC36.py's MODULE-LEVEL
    NEEDS_KERNEL, which refuses every arm of that file; the runnable twins live in the new module
    IC36_N.py (the pattern research/ideas/IC59_K.py documents for IC59).
  * IC59/A-C and IC54/A-B (bank_v3) are untouched and still blocked as bank_v3 recorded.

caveats
  1. Δ is arm − K on the same resampled paths and on the arm's own declared window; a Δ between
     two arms on different windows is not a comparison.
  2. Stage 1's pass rule is a strict `> 0`, so an arm equal to K would pass on rounding noise; none
     of these five is numerically K (all |AT2000 Δ| >= 0.49 pp).
  3. 20-session blocks cannot capture serial dependence beyond 20 sessions; the deflation's
     p-values are optimistic under long memory, and N = 312 is the whole campaign ledger.
  4. IC45/C_N's Stage-1 pass rests on a reduced window (2004-11-19 ->), where K itself compounds at
     19.70 % pre-tax against 15.76 % on the published window; its Δ is paired against K on the same
     window, but the level is not the published one.
  5. GLD/IEF start in 2004-11 / 2002-07, so every arm in this batch has no OOS window and almost no
     instrument window: a Stage-1 survivor here can never be more than `holdout_insufficient`.
