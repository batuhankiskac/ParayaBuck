IC59's original arms (research/ideas/IC59.py, pinned in REGISTRY.jsonl as needs_kernel) are left
byte-for-byte untouched; the runnable arms with the _K names live in research/ideas/IC59_K.py (see
that module's docstring for why: the module-level NEEDS_KERNEL applies to every arm of the module,
and re-hashing the old specs would make verify_ledger report spec_drift and refuse every batch).

rebuilds the leg from the window's own dividend column; IC40-C reads the already-registered `sp500`
(^GSPC) key of research/batch_screen/EXTRA_DATA.json.  Nothing added to EXTRA_DATA.json, no
DATA_MANIFEST entry: none of these arms needed a download.
