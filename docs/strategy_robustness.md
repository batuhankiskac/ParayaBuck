# Strategy robustness pack — operational summary

Non-optimizing robustness evidence for the two FROZEN validated strategies and
their combined portfolio. Generated by `scripts/strategy_robustness.py` and
`scripts/combined_portfolio_validation.py`; raw numbers live in
`reports/robustness/{equity,crypto,combined}.json` and `summary.md`
(gitignored). **Production parameters were NOT changed by this work** — the
frozen specs stay the production specs regardless of any diagnostic outcome.

## Scope and method

- Offline only: `data/historical/*.csv` through the production backtest
  harness (`parayabuck.backtest.product`), same PIT universe policy, same risk
  overlay, same cost model as paper/live. No broker calls, no paper/live
  orders (no `PARAYABUCK_ALLOW_PAPER_SMOKE`).
- Frozen specs: `equity_residual_momentum_v1` (formation 252 / skip 21 / beta
  273 / residual vol 63 / top_n 8 / max weight 0.10 / ivol quantile 0.70 /
  exclude [SPY], allocation 0.50) and `crypto_low_variance_v1`
  (variance_lookback_days 22 / top_n 2 / rebalance_hours 168, allocation
  0.10). Risk caps: crypto exposure 10%, crypto position 5%, crypto positions
  2, gross 60%, cash buffer 40%.
- Windows: equity 2017-01-03..2026-07-30 (2016 warmup); crypto 2024-12-01..
  2026-07-31 (2024-08..11 warmup), 1h scanner data aggregated to 1d.

## Combined portfolio (equity 0.50 + crypto 0.10)

Common window 2024-12-02..2026-07-30 (414 equity trading days); sleeves sized
independently against one 100k account (production semantics), combined value
= equity sleeve + crypto sleeve − initial cash (one shared cash pool).

| metric | equity sleeve | crypto sleeve | combined |
|---|---|---|---|
| CAGR | 8.05% | -1.03% | 7.37% |
| Sharpe | 0.71 | -0.18 | 0.59 |
| Sortino | 0.96 | -0.26 | 0.83 |
| maxDD | 7.62% | 7.59% | 9.50% |
| Calmar | 1.06 | -0.14 | 0.78 |
| avg exposure | 45.03% | 9.49% | 54.52% (cash 45.48%) |
| fees (% of avg equity) | 0.29% | 1.25% | 1.15% |
| turnover | 9.29 | 4.47 | 12.34 |

- Sleeve daily-PnL correlation: **0.35** — the crypto sleeve is a genuine
  diversifier (low correlation to equity-sleeve PnL).
- Risk-on fractions: both sleeves risk-on 95.7% of days, one sleeve risk-on
  4.3%, both cash-heavy 0.0% — the combination is almost always fully
  deployed within its caps.
- Benchmarks over the common window: SPY +22.9% (Sharpe 0.80), BTC -32.5%.

**Verdict:** crypto adds diversification (correlation 0.35), dilutes return
(combined CAGR 7.37% vs equity-only 8.05% — the crypto sleeve was slightly
negative over this window), and does not reduce max drawdown (combined 9.50%
vs equity-only 7.62%). It also adds cost drag (crypto fees ≈1.25% of avg
equity vs equity 0.29%). The allocation ratio is NOT optimized and stays
frozen; the combination's risk caps (gross 0.60 / cash 0.40 / crypto 0.10 /
0.05 / 2 positions) are verified by `tests/test_combined_account_risk.py`.

## Operational implications

1. The frozen parameters sit on a stable-to-moderately-sensitive plateau for
   equity (top_n is the only parameter that moves results meaningfully, and
   monotonically so) and a stable plateau for crypto. No parameter change is
   warranted by this evidence.
2. The equity strategy's realized edge concentrates in bull/high-vol regimes;
   the crypto sleeve's return concentrates in BTC-up regimes. Both are
   documented risks, not gates.
3. Cost headroom is very large for equity (break-even ≈24x costs) and negative
   for crypto at current levels — crypto is cost-sensitive by construction
   (25 bps taker on a small, slowly turning sleeve).
4. Bootstrap uncertainty bands are wide for both sleeves (equity ann-mean
   p5/p95 0.7%..11.3%; crypto -4.8%..+4.1%) — consistent with 2017-era equity
   and a 20-month crypto window, and a reminder that these are diagnostics,
   not guarantees.
5. No live enablement anywhere: `runtime.mode` remains `paper`; all safety
   gates are untouched.
