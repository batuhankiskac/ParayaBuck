# Event clock V2

The clock is the sorted union of timezone-aware execution-bar timestamps. At each timestamp, only signals with `bar.timestamp <= timestamp` are ingested. Equity uses its supplied NY-session date label (04:00/05:00 UTC according to DST); weekends and US holidays have no equity bar. Crypto uses complete UTC daily bars aggregated from supplied hourly bars and runs throughout weekends. The aggregator labels each complete daily bucket at its starting UTC midnight, even though its final hourly component is later in that UTC day. The next actual instrument bar supplies the next-open fill; no missing session is synthesized. On a crypto-only weekend tick, equity retains its last causally seen close mark.

**Material clock limitation:** both daily series carry labels earlier than physical completion: equity uses a New York session-date midnight label; crypto aggregation uses the UTC bucket start. The canonical isolated replay consumes each completed bar at its label. Preserving these labels is required by the frozen isolated hashes. Thus cross-market same-day slot priority can differ from a physically timed PAPER/LIVE sequence. V2 records and does not conceal this approximation; readiness depends on proving it immaterial or correcting it in a separate clock-parity study.
