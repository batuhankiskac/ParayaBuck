# SEC and issuer source audit

Retrieved 2026-09-22. This is a source design, not a claim of complete historical coverage.

## Verified filing path

1. Discover child Exchange Act registration under the child's CIK, typically `10-12B` for a national-exchange listed class and `10-12B/A` amendments. The [Solventum SEC index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964738/000162828024005591/0001628280-24-005591-index.htm) proves an actual `10-12B` accepted 2024-02-20 16:31:18 under child CIK **1964738**, accession **0001628280-24-005591**. Its [amendment index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964738/000162828024009297/0001628280-24-009297-index.htm) proves `10-12B/A` accession **0001628280-24-009297** accepted 2024-03-05 21:27:09 though its official filing date is 2024-03-06. This is a concrete reason to store both timestamps.
2. Read the applicable contemporaneous Form 10 and its information statement, often `EX-99.1`, for parent and child names, security class, distribution ratio/percentage, proposed record and distribution dates, expected when-issued and regular-way markets, exchange and ticker, conditions, tax treatment, retained stake, and amendments. [GRAIL Exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1699031/000119312524132022/d556103dex991.htm) proves that many such fields can be provisional/blank. A child may have been a reporting subsidiary already, so Form 10 is a strong common path, not an exhaustive legal requirement: SEC [Staff Legal Bulletin No. 4](https://www.sec.gov/interps/legal/slbcf4.txt) distinguishes previously non-reporting and reporting children.
3. Query both **parent and child** `8-K` filings and their exhibits for board declaration, effectiveness, completion and final trading schedule. Solventum's [pre-completion 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964738/000162828024010988/solventum-effectiveness8xk.htm) sets expected WI/RW dates, while its [completion 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964738/000162828024014887/solventum-closing8xk4324.htm) establishes realized completion but was filed after the first regular-way open. 3M's [EDGAR-filed release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/66740/000006674024000023/pressrelease3-8x24.htm) separately sets the trading schedule.
4. Treat SEC effectiveness notices and issuer statements as separate fields. An issuer's [acceleration request](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964738/000162828024010404/filename1.htm) requests effectiveness at a future time; it does **not** prove that the SEC granted it or that the distribution later occurred. Search the actual notice and completion sources.
5. Preserve all correction/amendment vintages. Later `10-Q`/`10-K` can reconcile event history but cannot retroactively qualify an earlier entry. A later SEC filing may confirm historical fact while being unavailable at an earlier decision point.

The filing header/index record must carry at least `accessionNumber`, submission `form`, `acceptanceDateTime` (or `<ACCEPTANCE-DATETIME>`), official `filingDate`, filer CIK, document list, and archive URL. Parse CIK from the **filer header**; the first ten digits of accession identify the submitting filer or agent and are **not automatically child CIK**. The [SEC EDGAR FAQ](https://www.sec.gov/about/webmaster-frequently-asked-questions) explicitly cautions on accession prefix and distinguishes acceptance date/time from filing and report-period dates. The [SEC EDGAR access guide](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) documents archive complete-submission files, headers, and indices. The [SEC APIs page](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) provides public submissions JSON and bulk submissions; discovery APIs are retrieval aids, not a substitute for archived originals and their submission headers.

## Fail-closed join

Candidate match requires source-proven parent legal issuer and CIK, child legal issuer and CIK, class description, ratio, then actual regular-way ticker/exchange date. A name search or `Form 10` hit alone yields `UNRESOLVED`. A parent `8-K` that merely announces contemplation is `PROPOSED`, not `COMPLETED`. No commercial corporate-action database or security master is assumed. Historical completeness, false positives, missing time stamps, issuer renames, and listing mapping success remain unmeasured pending a counts-only inventory.
