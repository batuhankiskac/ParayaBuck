# PAPER account ownership
engine.manage_external_positions is false. Reconciliation logs and leaves a position without ledger/portfolio ownership unchanged (alpaca_reconciliation.py:618-620); the bot does not adopt, grow, or intentionally sell it. Equity and crypto manual inventory nevertheless consume broker-wide gross and global/market slot capacity through account positions. Broker-wide OPEN orders count toward slot and BUY commitment capacity; unknown or unpriceable orders block entry. Manual equity/crypto left from another bot therefore reduce headroom, and quantity drift against a recorded managed position can halt.
Operator rule: use a fresh dedicated PAPER account if possible. At minimum inspect broker positions and OPEN orders before launch, resolve unexpected inventory manually outside this audit, and retain the matching runtime DB for any bot-owned inventory. Never delete the DB to make existing positions appear flat.
Sources: config/settings.yaml; alpaca_reconciliation.py:540-630; execution/portfolio.py:577-610; portfolio/runtime_service.py:1635-1742.
