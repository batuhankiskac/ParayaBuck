# Modern long-leg evidence

Verdict: `LONG_ONLY_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`

## What survives

- Brochet (2010) finds stronger filing-date reaction and volume for purchases
  after accelerated SOX disclosure. This establishes price relevance, not an
  outsider's next-open after-cost alpha.
- Biggerstaff, Cicero and Wintoki (2020) find positive alphas for several buy
  patterns through 2017, including CMP-opportunistic subsets. The tests are
  largely equal-weight and pattern-based; they do not validate a liquid
  CMP-only one-slot rule.
- A non-peer-reviewed 2008-2024 replication reports positive value-weight
  opportunistic-buy alpha, but routine buys are also significant and the
  opportunistic-minus-routine buy differences are insignificant. It is useful
  corroboration of generic buys, not decisive CMP incrementality.

## Evidence adverse to the requested mandate

- Lee, Lemmon, Li and Sequeira's 1986-2010 subperiod analysis reports that
  equal-weight purchase alpha survives, while value-weight purchase alpha from
  2003 onward is insignificant; the authors state that large-firm insiders do
  not earn abnormal purchase returns in that period.
- Gangopadhyay and Yook (2022) directly report that opportunistic-purchase
  profits are significantly lower after Dodd-Frank than before it.
- Oenschläger and Möllenhoff (2025) test outsiders reacting rapidly to public
  post-SOX Form 4 filings. Positive short-horizon percentage abnormal returns
  are smaller than older studies, negatively related to liquidity, and vanish
  or become negative when tradable dollar capacity is constrained—even before
  transaction costs.

## Gate assessment

1. CMP's purchase long leg is positive: **yes, historically**.
2. It is separate from the buy-minus-sell headline: **yes, but weaker**.
3. It survives value weighting/larger stocks: **partly in CMP; not robustly in
   later large/value-weight evidence**.
4. Modern post-publication evidence: **mixed and decayed**.
5. Delayed public execution: **causally possible; scalable after-cost support
   is absent**.
6. Small-stock dependence: **material concern**.

There is no peer-reviewed 2017-2026 result proving the incremental CMP
opportunistic-buy classification after realistic costs in a $20M-ADV liquid
universe, and no evidence for top-one concentration. The correct pre-outcome
decision is not “try the backtest”; it is failure of the external evidence gate.
