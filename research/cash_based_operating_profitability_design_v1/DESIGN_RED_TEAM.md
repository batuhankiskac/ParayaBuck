# Design red team

1. SEC XBRL is not Compustat; exact semantic equivalence is accession-specific.
2. Compustat missing-to-zero cannot be applied to an absent XBRL tag.
3. SG&A/R&D inclusion relationships can reverse the intended add-back.
4. Custom-tag dependence requires a predeclared semantic registry, not fuzzy NLP.
5. Prepaids, deferred revenue and accrued expenses are often aggregated.
6. Same-accession comparative facts are PIT-usable but may be restated.
7. Later restatements must not overwrite earlier decisions.
8. M&A and discontinued operations can break balance-sheet articulation.
9. CFS variables can reduce non-articulation but are a different specification.
10. Choosing CFS after observing missingness would be candidate substitution.
11. Filing-header SIC makes financial exclusion feasible, but missing SIC fails closed.
12. US-GAAP 10-K-only scope improves fidelity but changes the original universe.
13. It removes much ETF/ETN contamination, not all shell/REIT/multiclass risk.
14. Modern long-only evidence is materially weaker after selection adjustment.
15. Big-stock high-decile CAPM alpha is only borderline in the original table.
16. Generic quality/profitability evidence cannot be assigned to exact CbOP.
17. The research evidence relies on broad diversified portfolios.
18. Top-six concentration is not tested and amplifies single-filing error.
19. Top-one satellite alpha density has no external support.
20. Annual stale accounting may retain spread information but modern long-leg
    persistence is not established.
21. Equal weighting is an unvalidated departure; PIT value weights are unavailable.
22. The $5/$20m/top-300 scanner changes the paper universe and may remove the
    cross-section supporting the factor.
23. June-close/July-first-open is representable, but requires a custom annual
    research scheduler rather than the generic monthly path.
24. Lifecycle exits are representable, but merger consideration/successor
    mapping is not a complete annual-hold solution.

## Red-team conclusion

Source engineering could create a narrow high-fidelity panel, but it cannot
repair the independent evidence failure: the published characteristic is a
diversified factor, not a validated six-name or one-name ParayaBuck strategy.
