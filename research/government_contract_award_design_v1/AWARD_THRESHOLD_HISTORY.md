# Effective-dated announcement thresholds

| Effective period | FAR 5.303 federal announcement | DoD DFARS 205.303 announcement | Provenance |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2016-01-01 to 2020-09-30 | $4m | $7m | [FAR archive](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/archives/pdf/FAC_2020-04.pdf); [2015 DoD rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-06-26/pdf/2015-15668.pdf) |
| 2020-10-01 to 2025-09-30 | $4.5m | $7.5m | [FAR 2020 change](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/archives/pdf/2020-09.pdf); [DFARS 2020 archive](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/archives/pdf/DFARS_Change_10-01-2020.pdf) |
| 2025-10-01 onward | $5.5m | $9m | [FAR threshold changes](https://www.acquisition.gov/threshold-changes); [DFARS 205.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/subpart-205.3-synopses-contract-awards) |

The sources establish covered-agency headline thresholds, not identical web-publishing practices across agencies. [FAR 5.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/5.303) has exceptions, including sensitive or otherwise withheld information. [DFARS 205.303](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/subpart-205.3-synopses-contract-awards) includes modifications and special face-value/IDIQ rules. A future sample must apply the rule effective on each action's date and explicitly compare event supply across threshold regimes. A static present-day $9m DoD filter would create retrospective selection.
