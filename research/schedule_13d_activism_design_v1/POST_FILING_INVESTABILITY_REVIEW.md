# Post-filing investability review

## Causal clock

The activist accumulates before public disclosure. A public outsider's earliest admissible information time is conservative dissemination after EDGAR acceptance, and the earliest admissible execution is the following eligible regular-session open. No pre-filing, filing-date-close, or assumed instantaneous filing-day fill belongs to the outsider strategy.

## What the evidence actually supports

- Brav et al.'s only statistically positive post-event slice is equal-weighted months +1 to +3. The value-weighted estimate for the same window is economically small and statistically null; later value-weighted windows are null.
- Broad announcement windows combine accumulation/leakage, disclosure reaction, and sometimes subsequent days. Their total cannot be assigned to a next-open trade.
- Long-horizon work mainly rejects reversal. Insignificant positive estimates are not proof of a positive implementable edge.
- Engagement-lifetime studies frequently begin before public disclosure or realize gains at later outcomes. Exit and outcome dates are unknown at entry.
- Studies' filing-date daily windows do not implement a raw acceptance timestamp, dissemination lag, exchange calendar, next regular open, or after-hours rule.

## Holding-period assessment

| Candidate horizon | Evidence problem |
|---|---|
| Next day | Announcement timing is not separated from implementable fill timing |
| 1–3 months | Positive only in an equal-weighted legacy sample; value-weighted result is null |
| 1–5 years | No robust calendar-time alpha; “no reversal” is not positive edge |
| Until activist exit | Exit is future information and published deal returns include pre-filing time |
| Until outcome/takeover | Direct outcome lookahead |

A 63-trading-day horizon would be the least arbitrary hostile replication of the one positive equal-weighted window, but the user requires evidence before authorizing economics. It is therefore not frozen or tested.

## Gate verdict

`OUTSIDER_INVESTABLE_POST_FILING_LONG_LEG_NOT_SUPPORTED`

Per the requested gate, this rejects the strategy before local economic testing.
