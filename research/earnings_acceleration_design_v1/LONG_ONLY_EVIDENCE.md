# Long-only evidence

The [author draft Table 2](https://www.naaim.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/2018_00M_Earnings-Acceleration-and-Stock-Returns_NAAIM-Submission-with-title-page.pdf) reports high EAP decile market-adjusted returns of 1.6% over day 2-30 (t=5.131) and 4.2% over the quarter-long window (t=6.19) for equal weighting. Value-weighted high-decile returns are 0.8% (t=2.985) and 0.8% (t=1.921); the quarter-long value-weighted long leg is not convincingly different from zero. The 1.8%/3.4% headline values are high-minus-low spreads. Table 3 reports risk-adjusted decile portfolios, but none represents six or fewer names.

Draft section 4.3 reports 2004-2015 positive hedge quarters in 41/48 and spread persistence after excluding sub-$5 and up-to-$0.5B-cap stocks. These are spread robustness results, not independently verified long-leg results under those restrictions. The published [abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165410119300333) repeats the low-price and low-cap claim. Evidence supports diversified long-high as a historical U.S. result, with weaker value-weighted evidence at the longer horizon. It does not support a few-name long-only expression.
