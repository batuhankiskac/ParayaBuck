# Monitoring before PAPER rollout

Dashboard `_dashboard_risk` exposes realized gross, cash ratio, equity and crypto sleeve values, configured gross/cash/crypto limits, max positions, crypto count, risk latch, breaker and incidents (`src/parayabuck/web/server_legacy.py:3060-3095`). PAPER observability emits `risk_overlay_gross_after` (`portfolio/runtime_service.py:409`). Orders and decisions are persisted and no-trade capacity can include gross before/after. However the reviewed risk panel does not directly expose effective occupied-plus-reserved slots, account-wide working-order count or a single aligned target-versus-realized gross view. The source data may be queryable from ledgers but current operator view is incomplete. Instrument those values and explicit over-limit BUY suppression before PAPER rollout; monitoring alone cannot compensate for the submission defect. No post-hoc gross tolerance is proposed.
