# Relationship availability

Research record must store `accession`, raw-document SHA-256, fiscal period, SEC `accepted_at` (UTC), the public dissemination rule, identity evidence and dated security mapping. Earliest usability is no earlier than the first **proved** public release of the actual customer identity. For a February 2022 10-K describing FY2021, no 2021 decision may use it absent an earlier source. The original paper instead imposes a conservative six-month fiscal-year-end gap; a public reconstruction should satisfy both the original lag and observed filing availability rather than backdate to fiscal-year start.

An amendment creates a new vintage. A later disclosed identity cannot be inserted into earlier anonymous observations. When accepted_at or source version is missing, edge eligibility is `UNKNOWN` and fails closed. No live edge records were created here.
