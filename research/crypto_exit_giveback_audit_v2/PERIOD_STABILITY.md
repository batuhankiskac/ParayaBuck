# Period stability

The canonical full replay is the authority. No canonically defined independent development/validation split is available for this giveback audit; archived overlapping half-year folds are not relabeled as such. No parameter was selected from these results.

Annual cohorts below assign naturally completed episodes by exit year. They are realized episode cohorts, not annual marked portfolio returns; cross-year economic paths remain intact. 2021 and 2026 are partial years.

Full-period natural results: 55 episodes, 50 positive MFE, 20 winner-to-loser round trips, 6,009.561372 net P&L, median positive-MFE retention 37.27%. The >=30% group has 15 episodes and median retention 87.15%.

Retention varies considerably by year, and the large-winner annual samples are too small to claim stable independent validation. Nonetheless, large winners were preserved in multiple years rather than uniformly surrendering most gains. The combined evidence supports mixed behavior, not a demonstrated typical large-winner collapse. Entry-year cohort sensitivity is separately tabulated in INTERPRETATION_REVIEW.md; it is explicitly a different attribution convention.
