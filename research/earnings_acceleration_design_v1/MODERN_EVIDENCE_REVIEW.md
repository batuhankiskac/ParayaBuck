# Modern evidence review

Classification: `NO_MEANINGFUL_MODERN_CONFIRMATION` for post-2015 U.S. concentrated long-only EAP. The original U.S. sample ends in 2015; its 2004-2015 internal robustness is not a post-publication test. A 2020 [Swedish independent thesis](https://arc.hhs.se/download.aspx?MediumId=4821), using 2004-2016 Swedish data, reports no clear decile progression and negative high-leg abnormal returns over its 30- and 90-day windows. It is an international counterexample, not a contemporary U.S. replication, and methodology/market differences limit transfer. Search found later work on *disaggregated* acceleration, not a direct EAP replication; it is not treated as confirmation. Evidence staleness raises the burden for a later frozen economic test, but no local returns are consulted here.
