# Gross policy audit

Classification: `MAXIMUM_GROSS_IS_TARGET_LIMIT`.

`docs/research/canonical_v3_baseline.md:35-68` explicitly distinguishes the risk-adjusted expected post-trade target from marked realized exposure. Target gross is the sum of absolute intended post-trade positions divided by equity, including immutable external inventory once. Realized gross is `sum(abs(qty)*current_price)/equity`; `PositionSnapshot.market_value` implements the absolute value (`src/parayabuck/core/models.py:197-198`). The policy has no continuous drift-triggered liquidation. It explicitly requires every BUY submission/re-plan to use a fresh account snapshot and stay within available broker gross headroom.

`config/settings.yaml:179-185` sets gross 0.60 and cash buffer 0.40. `portfolio_risk_limits_from_config` maps both to PAPER/LIVE (`src/parayabuck/execution/portfolio.py:112-125`). Overlay `apply` counts marked positions and immutable external exposure, caps managed target by gross and cash limits, and reports `gross_before`/`gross_after` (`portfolio.py:158-422`). `gross_after` is an expected target, not a broker fill observation. The target-to-order planner suppresses deltas below 0.005 (`portfolio.py:425-554`). Deferred buys use a fresh snapshot and `_size_buys_to_safe_capacity` (`runtime_service.py:1399-1453,1543-1609`). Pure buys bypass that check (`runtime_service.py:1180-1209`). This violates the documented order-caused exposure clause even when the risk-adjusted target remains at 0.60.

Held appreciation, asymmetric equity/crypto marks, NAV changes, costs and fill timing can move realized gross above target absent a target violation; those are possible mechanisms under the policy. The frozen trace establishes marked drift and fill-adjacent versus later onsets, but does not uniquely assign each change to those separate mechanisms. Historical abnormal-volume G9 explicitly tested realized gross and remains failed; the later clarification is prospective.
