# Stage C source and concentration feasibility

Three candidates warranted deeper official-source inspection. None reached the shortlist. This is documentation/sample-index inspection, not a historical dataset build or event-count calculation.

- **V4-021 abnormal institutional ownership** — REJECT_CONCENTRATION: Published abstract reports high-minus-low only; no independently evidenced few-name high tail. Characteristic model and historical 13F security mapping remain unresolved.
- **V4-031 Phase III failed-trial rebound** — REJECT_CONCENTRATION: 2025 paper has only 92 events and the long-friendly rebound is small-firm failure dependent; liquid, few-name post-disclosure evidence absent.
- **V4-033 FDA fast-track designation** — REJECT_CONCENTRATION: Recent 25-event study reports long-horizon gains but has a narrow 2019-2020 sample; liquid few-name replication, PIT sponsor mapping and independent modern confirmation absent.

13F: the SEC official structured bulk spans July 2013–August 2026 and is republished quarterly. An inspected original filing index, accession 0001275218-26-000017, identifies period 2026-06-30, filing date 2026-08-13, acceptance 17:12:07, and an XML information table. That directly demonstrates why report period cannot be `available_at`. CUSIP/class still need original-filing availability and historical security mapping. A 45-day quarter-end lag is not equivalent to each individual filing timestamp. A published decile spread does not justify selecting six names. Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1275218/000127521826000017/0001275218-26-000017-index.htm and https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/form-13f-data-sets

Phase III: ClinicalTrials.gov exposes current study data and a history of posted versions; trial registry dates cannot be substituted for first issuer disclosure. FDA approval data identify applications and applicants, not a PIT listed-security map. The recent paper describes 92 events, with the long-friendly failure rebound concentrated in small firms; no liquid six-slot case. Sources: https://clinicaltrials.gov/submit-studies/prs-help/how-submit-results and https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/about-drugsfda

Fast Track: FDA program documentation establishes the regulatory category and says the sponsor requests designation, but a designation decision date is not a first public tradable announcement. Williamson and Spicer examined 25 biotech announcements in June 2019–June 2020; no separately validated liquid high-strength subgroup or enough concurrent slot supply is reported. Sources: https://www.fda.gov/patients/fast-track-breakthrough-therapy-accelerated-approval-priority-review/fast-track and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1359644623002878
