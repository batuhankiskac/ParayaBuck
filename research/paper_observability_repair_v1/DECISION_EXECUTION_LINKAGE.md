# Decision to execution linkage

`portfolio_decisions.decision_id` owns the target and risk decision. `portfolio_orders.decision_id` owns submission status, broker order ID and cumulative fill state. `portfolio_fills.client_order_id` owns broker activity fills. `portfolio_execution_events.client_order_id` stores raw event history with dedup identity. `portfolio_rebalance_executions.decision_id` owns delayed SELL to BUY phase state. V2 receipts mirror the first decision moment and point to those tables. `portfolio_buy_admission_observations` has append-only observation IDs and a client-order link for every final fresh BUY admission attempt; the latest observation before the submission call is the relevant gate. Missing linked rows or unresolved ambiguity fail the soak auditor.
