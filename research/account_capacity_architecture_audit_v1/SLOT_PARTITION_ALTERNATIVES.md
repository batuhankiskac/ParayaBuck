# Slot partition alternatives

All alternatives are design comparisons only. They retain the existing 0.60 account gross, 0.40 target cash minimum, 0.55 equity and 0.05 crypto managed sleeves, 0.10 equity and 0.05 crypto per-name limits, eligibility gates, and the two-crypto-name limit unless expressly stated. No configuration change or migration test is part of this audit.

| Architecture | Structural effect | Benefit | Cost or unresolved question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Current global six | Six distinct cross-market symbols/claims share one ceiling. Two crypto claims leave four equity names. | Small explicit order/monitoring cardinality; simple account-level backstop. | Full equity sleeve cannot be expressed with two crypto slots; six-equity research is only conditional; one small crypto target displaces a larger potential equity target by one slot. |
| B. Higher global cap, e.g. eight or ten | At eight, six equities plus two crypto names fit while all weight caps remain. At ten, V3's current top-eight equities plus two crypto names fit. | Restores possible full 0.55/0.05 sleeve expression at eight; simple continuation of global accounting. | More targets and working orders; no minimum position weight; ten adds two equity names beyond the six required merely for 0.55 capacity and needs an independent reason. |
| C. Sleeve-specific count caps | Equity count and crypto count are independently bounded, while gross remains shared. A conceptual six-equity/two-crypto design has a maximum of eight distinct names. | Cardinality reflects different sleeve sizes and execution mechanics; crypto cannot crowd equity count by construction. | Requires cross-market total order/monitoring backstop, migration of overlay and research contracts, and explicit handling of external inventory. A conceptual eight-name maximum still exists even if unstated. |
| D. Explicit reservations | Strategies atomically reserve slots before target admission and release them after an exit is confirmed. | Makes simultaneous decisions and research budgets visible, reducing ambiguity over pending claims. | More state and recovery logic: expired/canceled orders, partial fills, failed exits, and crash replay. A reservation policy still needs a chosen total or per-sleeve cardinality. |
| E. No hard count | Gross/sleeve/per-name, minimum order/notional, and liquidity rules alone limit exposure. | Removes count-driven underdeployment. | The existing $10 order floor is an order threshold, not a position-weight floor. Current strategy `top_n` still bounds ordinary positive targets to eight equity plus two crypto, but future strategies and external inventory would lack a separate account count ceiling. Reconciliation/API/monitoring load loses that deterministic backstop. |

The geometry threshold is eight for *possible* simultaneous expression of six equity names needed to reach 0.55 under a 0.10 per-name cap plus two crypto names. This is a capacity statement only. Eight is not proved optimal for risk, returns, or operations. A future migration study would need to freeze the target count, simultaneous order and external-inventory semantics, plus a minimum meaningful target/order policy before production adoption.
