# Risk state on restart

\`AccountService.account\` calls the account-scoped peak reconciliation service and sets snapshot peak to max(persisted peak, current broker equity) (\`alpaca_account.py:225-251\`; \`risk_peak.py:246-370\`). \`EngineRuntime._persist_risk_state\` saves day-start equity as current equity minus broker daily P&L, peak/epoch, realized P&L, loss count, and circuit reason (\`runtime.py:4100-4165\`; \`ledger.py:1432-1461\`). \`_restore_persistent_state\` restores incident reasons across days and today's loss counter; daily baseline deliberately resets on a new UTC session (\`runtime.py:2327-2408\`). Drawdown halt is a persistent incident until a verified reset, and the first fresh account poll re-evaluates the latch.

The ledger loader treats corrupt \`risk_state\` JSON as absent (\`ledger.py:1463-1498\`); this may reset session loss count, while a separately persisted safety incident survives. A corrupt **incident table** or state write failure was not tested. There is no evidence of a normal same-day restart clearing an already-persisted drawdown incident. Existing \`test_safety_incidents.py\` and restart suites exercise those paths. A crash between observing a breach and persisting its incident remains a general durability window; the fresh broker account/peak pass should rediscover an ongoing breach, but the audit did not certify every instantaneous equity reversal sequence.
