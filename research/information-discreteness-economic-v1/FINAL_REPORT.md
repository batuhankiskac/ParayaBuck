# Information Discreteness Economic v1

Decision: `RESEARCH_REJECT`

This was one frozen research-only economic experiment with a fresh contemporaneous Control A (`equity_residual_momentum_v3`) and the frozen Candidate B. Both arms used one continuous state path, the same PIT scanner, roles, capital, execution engine, and 1x/2x/3x cost stress. Candidate B used the exact last-completed regular-session monthly cadence.

The independent verifier recomputed 2,911 scoreable signal records across 372 symbols, all six persisted result curves, exact date boundaries, fill causality, reserved roles, target caps, and cost monotonicity.

Gate outcomes are in `GATE_TABLE.json`; period metrics are in `METRICS.json`; calendar-year validation is in `YEARLY_VALIDATION.json`; diagnostics are in `DECISION_COVERAGE.json`, `OVERLAP_DIAGNOSTICS.json`, `TURNOVER_DIAGNOSTICS.json`, and `CONCENTRATION_DIAGNOSTICS.json`.

No production, PAPER, or LIVE configuration was changed. The final decision is not a promotion or auto-promotion instruction.
