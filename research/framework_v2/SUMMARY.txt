WHAT WAS ADDED (task goals 1-3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. REDUCED WINDOWS. spec["start"] = "auto" | "YYYY-MM-DD" (plus spec["inputs"]).
   Stage 1, stage 2 and both block bootstraps run on the declared window, with K
   re-simulated on the SAME window as control (per-window cache _control_<tag>.json).
   Rules (README §1a, PREREGISTRATION §6.2): "auto" = the later of the published start
   and the first session every declared input covers, plus one session (the engine reads
   the previous close at i0-1); a window shorter than 10 calendar years is
   NotApplicable; the three after-tax windows are clipped up to the window's start (no
   key dropped, the record names the window that ran); the January rolling starts that
   begin before it are dropped (<15 leaves the arm holdout_insufficient); the block
   bootstrap draws from the window's own pool at the published seed/block/rng order; the
   OOS windows, the 21 ETFs and the pooled OOS bootstrap are unchanged (other markets).
   The record / scoreboard / DEFLATION.json carry the window; an arm that declares
   nothing takes the published code path (default pool bit-identical to pool=None).
2. THE REPORTED BUG. MarketData.leg_b_usable indexed self.days with a column index; on a
   bootstrap chunk the market arrays carry one artificial leading base row, so the
   reported session was off by one and the span string raised IndexError whenever the
   last column was finite. Reproduced with probes/two_leg.py::gld_sleeve and with the
   FRED key dgs10 (interior NaN) before the fix; fixed by MarketData.column_days (which
   also makes year/month (T,)); regression test + a harness cell.
3. TWO-LEG OPTIONS (copy: research/fast_engine2/fe2_opts.py; fe2_sim/fe2_boot untouched
   except one pure refactor). (a) The off-state ideas IC31-IC37 need NO per-session
   instrument switch: a fixed leg B whose weight varies is enough, demonstrated end to
   end by probes/two_leg.py::gld_offstate (start="auto") and ::onex_offstate
   (leg_b="1x", IC38) on real data through the plugin path. (b) gain-aware fill
   options: band_score usd|indexed|max (K's widths keyed to the TRY-indexed FIFO gain
   fraction), band_step4_pp (fourth step above a fixed 0.15), route_cap_pp (downsize
   routing to the cheaper-indexed leg, unit-based, capped by the cap / the other leg's
   sale / the cheap leg's remaining units), loss_net_frac + loss_net_min_ytd_try
   (loss-lot harvesting with a YTD gate, taxed modes only), kernel_scope
   after_tax_stage_only (IC42/IC43 keep the published kernel pre-tax). (c) three
   instruments in one account (IC36, IC34 arm C) is marked NEEDS_KERNEL, with the
   reason (a derived blend series would hide the sleeve's internal rescale from the
   taxpayer and leave it without a per-instrument FIFO book).

CAVEATS
  * A reduced-window arm's delta is paired against K on its own window; different windows
    do not share a pool, so its deflation p-value is not the published single-pool one
    (DEFLATION.json names the pools in window_tags; the compared quantity is the paired
    advantage, which is what the screening decision uses).
  * A two-leg sleeve realising a large TRY-indexed gain can leave an advance tax the
    account cannot fund on a few drawn paths: (NAV < 0)**(1/years) is NaN. Seen once, on
    the naive gld_offstate fixture (3 of 200 paths, progressive mode only); the arm fails
    closed at stage 1 and is reported in excluded_non_finite instead of poisoning every
    other arm's p-value. The single-leg control and the split arm are clean on that pool.
  * route_cap moves UNITS, so a routed fill's cash differs from the un-routed fill by the
    two instruments' price ratio; the band widths a routed leg is judged on are those its
    routed quantity earns.
  * No new data was downloaded; DATA_MANIFEST.json lists the seven inputs the new code
    touches with their SHA-256. research/fast_engine/ is untouched (the harness restores
    PARITY.json to its committed bytes).
